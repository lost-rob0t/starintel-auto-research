* Research Seed

Inspect current StarIntel durable outbox/event publication, idempotency/replay, CouchDB CAS/revision safety, authorization and event visibility, integration registry work, RabbitMQ/actor interfaces, API/OpenAPI contracts, observability, retry/terminal-failure conventions, and deterministic serialization/signature facilities before proposing webhook architecture.

External evidence should cover signing/freshness, retries, SSRF/redirect defenses, secret rotation, replay resistance, and idempotency patterns only where needed for the StarIntel decision.

* Research Log

** RESEARCHING Threat and reliability questions

The research must answer atomic mutation/outbox publication, crash recovery, duplicate producer submissions, restart-safe delivery, deterministic signed bytes, forged/stale/replayed requests, bounded payload/timeouts/redirects, SSRF prevention, destination references versus arbitrary URLs, retryable/permanent failure and dead-letter semantics, authorization separation, confused-deputy risks, and observability redaction.

** RESEARCHING Architecture boundaries

Keep tek9 graph/KB-only, preserve the CouchDB-backed Prolog query plane, keep Node-RED downstream rather than authoritative, and separate public webhook gateway topology from server-owned event semantics.
