* REVIEW Objective

Define one durable national-compatible contract for Ohio state systems, county systems, components, delegated vendor portals, shared deployments, static files, APIs, dashboards, form-driven searches, document archives, applicability decisions, independent reviews, atomic coverage gates, and explicit gaps without creating a schema fork per county.

* REVIEW Invariants

1. Search and adapter output creates proposals, never verified canonical state.
2. Every canonical profile, deployment, search plan, subfamily decision, gap, route assessment, review decision, and coverage gate is immutable and revisioned.
3. Every subject-state transition creates a new subject revision under its owning authority.
4. Human review never mutates the reviewed subject. It creates a separate review-decision revision owned only by the Independent Review Authority.
5. Completion references exact subject and review-decision revisions.
6. Every revision declares logical ID, revision ID, immediate predecessor, and superseded revisions.
7. Route assessments bind exact profile, deployment, search-plan, subfamily-decision, gap, evidence, policy, and applicability-contract revisions.
8. Jurisdiction and wave completion is represented by one atomic gate revision binding the exact route-assessment and review-decision revisions considered together.
9. Shared deployments are separate from jurisdiction-specific authority and scope.
10. A source profile does not imply route coverage.
11. Negative findings are evidence-bearing and expiring.
12. =:not-applicable= requires positive authority or service-topology evidence.
13. Current, historical, and future-effective content are measured separately.
14. Later revisions never change the basis of older decisions.
15. Browser and OCR output needs replayable artifact boundaries.
16. Personal-data and access restrictions are recorded before acquisition.

* REVIEW Official and Subject States

=:official-status= is =:verified-official=, =:verified-delegated=, =:candidate-official=, =:candidate-delegated=, =:unverified=, =:rejected=, or =:retired=.

Verified official/delegated states require government or authoritative evidence. Logos, names, certificates, and search results are insufficient alone.

Source states are =:candidate=, =:observed=, =:active=, =:changed=, =:blocked=, =:retired=, and =:rejected=.

Each source-state transition creates a new profile or deployment revision under its owning authority. Human review is not stored as mutable state inside the subject revision.

* REVIEW Independent Review Decision Revision

#+begin_src lisp
(defstruct review-decision-revision
  review-id review-revision-id prior-review-revision-id
  supersedes-review-revision-ids
  subject-kind subject-revision-id
  decision reviewer-id reviewer-generation
  independence-policy-id conflict-check-result
  evidence-ids notes decided-at created-at)
#+end_src

=subject-kind= includes deployment, profile, applicability contract, search plan, gap, subfamily decision, route assessment, and coverage gate.

=decision= is =:reviewed=, =:changes-requested=, =:invalidated=, or =:rejected=.

Rules:

- only the Independent Review Authority commits review-decision revisions
- the subject owner cannot self-review
- reviewer identity, generation, and independence-policy result are pinned
- a later review creates a new review revision; it never mutates the subject or prior review
- a reviewed subject becomes current only through a materialized view joining the immutable subject revision with its latest applicable review-decision revision
- invalidating a review does not delete or rewrite the subject

Every subject used by completion requires a separate reviewed review-decision revision.

Search-plan scope, state, contract, policy, terms, limits, or evidence changes create a new revision. Negative decisions require a completed search plan and current gap revision.

Route states are unassessed, candidate-found, verified, verified-partial, not-published, records-request-required, authentication-required, paywalled, blocked, retired, ambiguous, and not-applicable. Candidate, ambiguous, and unassessed are nonterminal.

Verified requires every required subfamily to have a reviewed positive or contract-permitted not-applicable decision. Verified-partial requires explicit missing scope and current reviewed gaps.

Default maximum negative ages are 30 days high, 90 days medium, 180 days low, and next effective date or 180 days for legal-versioned sources.

* REVIEW Atomic Coverage Gate Revision

#+begin_src lisp
(defstruct coverage-gate-revision
  gate-id gate-revision-id prior-gate-revision-id
  supersedes-gate-revision-ids
  gate-kind scope-id run-id wave-id
  applicability-contract-id applicability-contract-version
  applicability-contract-review-revision-id
  policy-snapshot-id adapter-manifest-set-hash runtime-environment-set-hash
  route-assessment-revision-ids route-assessment-review-revision-ids
  required-subject-review-revision-ids
  blocker-ids status basis-hash
  decided-at decider-id created-at)
#+end_src

=gate-kind= is =:jurisdiction-completion=, =:wave-exit=, or =:state-completion=.

=status= is =:passed=, =:blocked=, =:expired=, or =:superseded=.

A gate is atomic:

- it pins the exact set of all sixteen route-assessment revisions and their review-decision revisions
- it pins the applicability-contract review, policy, manifest/runtime set, and all required subject reviews
- =basis-hash= is calculated over the ordered bound revision set
- route/profile/gap changes after gate creation do not rewrite it
- expiry, contract migration, changed evidence, or invalidated reviews create a new gate revision
- only a reviewed =:passed= jurisdiction-completion gate may satisfy catalog =DONE=

The gate decider cannot be the reviewer who approves the gate review decision when independence policy requires separation.

* REVIEW Validation Rules

A subject revision is invalid when its owner, predecessor/supersession, authority, scope, contract, policy, evidence, or artifact requirements are missing.

A review decision is invalid when:

- the subject revision does not exist or is mutable
- the reviewer conflicts with proposing/owning identities under the independence policy
- reviewer identity/generation or conflict result is missing
- the review attempts to mutate the subject
- the same review revision is reused for a changed subject

A route assessment is invalid when required reviewed decisions or current reviewed gaps/search plans are missing, nonterminal states pass, operational metadata is incomplete, or old revisions are mutated.

A gate is invalid when:

- it does not bind all sixteen exact route-assessment and review revisions
- any required subject review is missing, invalidated, or stale
- contract/policy/manifest/runtime bindings are absent
- negative evidence is expired at =decided-at=
- =basis-hash= does not match the canonical ordered revision set
- a blocked or expired gate is used for =DONE=
- the gate or gate review is self-approved contrary to policy

* REVIEW Parent Protocol Requirement

=STAR-RESEARCH-PIPELINE-004= must be approved and reconciled with =STAR-RESEARCH-PIPELINE-003= before implementation. The protocol must provide review-decision and coverage-gate authorities and exact revision-aware messages.

* REVIEW Versioning and Migration

- logical IDs remain stable; revision IDs are immutable
- all subject and review transitions create new revisions
- review decisions are separate from subjects
- every superseding revision names what it replaces
- migrations create new locator/deployment/profile revisions
- retired revisions remain queryable
- contract/policy migration forces new decisions and gates
- latest-state materialization never erases historical basis

* REVIEW Acceptance Criteria

1. One revisioned schema covers state, county, component, and shared deployments.
2. All sixteen routes have machine-readable requirements.
3. Operational metadata is derived and cross-cutting.
4. Human review is a separate immutable record under one authority.
5. Subject owners never write review decisions.
6. Route assessments pin exact subject revisions.
7. Jurisdiction and wave results use atomic coverage-gate revisions.
8. Catalog =DONE= requires a reviewed passed gate.
9. Not-applicable requires positive evidence.
10. Negative findings expire.
11. Browser/OCR boundaries are reproducible.
12. PIPELINE-004 alignment is required before implementation.

* REVIEW Required Fixtures

- reviewed profile without subject mutation
- self-review and reviewer-conflict rejection
- invalidated review leaving the subject intact
- full sixteen-route jurisdiction gate
- one route changing after an old gate without changing old replay
- gate expiry after negative evidence becomes stale
- gate supersession after contract/policy migration
- basis-hash mismatch rejection
- coverage observations without a reviewed gate cannot produce =DONE=
- profile/gap/decision/assessment/review/gate supersession replay
