* Implementation Order

1. Review and approve or reject the research conclusion in =STAR-PRO-RESEARCH-009=.
2. Design a narrow federal opportunity collector and customer-independent change record.
3. Validate the official source with fixtures, a bounded credentialed probe, and a small customer interview/pilot set.
4. Add verified award context only when source identifiers support reliable joins.
5. Consider SEC filing signals after a buyer segment and differentiation test.

No revenue estimate, launch date, or source permission beyond the cited API contracts is implied by this order. The research is pending approval and no actor is advertised as implemented.

* Research Gaps

- Which contractor segment has a costly enough missed-opportunity problem to pay for alerts?
- What are the current SAM.gov API-key eligibility and downstream redistribution terms for the intended service?
- Which notice identifiers survive edits, cancellations, and archival transitions in observed data?
- How many award-to-opportunity joins can be verified without name-only inference?
- What retention and customer-isolation controls are required for watchlists and generated analyses?

* Footnotes and Glossary

- *Revenue hypothesis* — A testable claim that a specific customer will pay for a defined decision improvement; it is not a revenue forecast.
- *Source-independent projection* — A customer-facing filter over canonical source records that does not change the underlying collection history.
