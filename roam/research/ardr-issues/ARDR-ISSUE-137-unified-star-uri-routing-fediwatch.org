* Research Seed

Generalize `star://` from actor-only identity into authority plus resource-kind URIs; define the STAR application envelope independently from transport; redesign StarRouter as authority-aware resolver/router; register `starintel-gserver` under its own authority; reconcile with existing StarLang routing work; and audit whether FediWatch should use RabbitMQ for durable target intake/document output rather than ZeroMQ PUB/SUB semantics.
