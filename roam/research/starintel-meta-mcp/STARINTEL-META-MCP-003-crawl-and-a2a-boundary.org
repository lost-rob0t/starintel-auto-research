* Research question
Where should crawl end and autonomous research begin, given MCP task support and a future StarIntel A2A agent with access to both Meta MCP and the full StarIntel MCP?

* Working boundary to validate
- search: bounded discovery.
- fetch: retrieve a known resource.
- crawl: bounded deterministic traversal/indexing; may be long-running.
- research: iterative reasoning, gap analysis, branch selection, and stopping criteria; belongs to the future A2A agent.

* Research backlog
- MCP task semantics for long-running crawl.
- Cancellation, progress, result handles, and partial failure.
- A2A task/artifact semantics for research output.
- Crawl/index retention versus canonical StarIntel ingest.
- Permissions and safety boundaries between retrieval, crawl, and StarIntel write actions.
