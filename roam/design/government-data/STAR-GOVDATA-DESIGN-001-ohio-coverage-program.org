* REVIEW Objective

Make Ohio the first state executed from the generated jurisdiction catalog.

The program produces immutable subject revisions, CAS-linear independent review decisions, and time-bounded atomic coverage gates for Ohio and all 88 counties. It records authority, source identity, methods, scope, refresh, provenance, applicability, gaps, and historical relationships.

This is a bounded design, not a claim of complete coverage or a production runtime implementation.

* REVIEW Architecture

Ohio is one state-scoped run over reusable actors/adapters. Governments and components are records and bounded job keys, not permanent actors.

Required before live work:

- Identity, Deployment, Profile, Search Plan, Gap, Route Assessment, Coverage Gate, and Independent Review authorities
- Raw Artifact Authority, retrieval-event ledger, Provenance Validator, and Coverage Ledger
- Official Domain Resolver, Platform Fingerprint pool, Adapter Registry, bounded workers
- Rate/Lease Authority with per-attempt generation/fencing

Subject writers, review writer, and gate writer are disjoint. Coverage Ledger cannot synthesize completion.

=PIPELINE-004= must be approved with =PIPELINE-003= before implementation.

* REVIEW Scope

Included:

- State of Ohio and 88 county GEOIDs
- official/delegated state, county, component, shared, and regional systems
- all sixteen routes in =DESIGN-002=
- Franklin County first complete pilot
- fingerprint-driven diverse-county validation
- visible predecessor/successor/migration/archive/time relationships in every wave
- immutable reviews and atomic jurisdiction/wave/state gates

Deferred:

- exhaustive municipality/township/school/special-district coverage
- production crawling/normalization
- automatic records requests
- credential creation, terms acceptance, payment, or control bypass
- unauthorized protected data
- exhaustive historical crawling beyond policy
- continuous scheduling before pilot acceptance

* REVIEW Identity and State Source Seeds

The identity set includes Ohio, 88 counties, and discovered auditors, recorders, clerks, election boards, sheriffs, engineers, treasurers, courts, commissions, health districts, and planning bodies.

Each component records parent, function, authority, service geography, evidence, confidence, and county/elected/shared/regional/municipal/state/delegated applicability.

State seeds include Ohio portal identity, Ohio Laws, Legislature, OhioBuys, Ohio Checkbook, OGRIP/OSIP/LBRS, Secretary of State, Supreme Court, Auditor of State, archives/retention, and records/open-meetings law.

Every promoted source requires authority evidence. Ohio Checkbook is discovery/reconciliation evidence only.

* REVIEW Route and Historical Contracts

All route semantics come from one independently reviewed applicability-contract revision. State substitution requires explicit rule permission and proved county/component/period scope.

Every wave records first/last observations, predecessor/successor locators, redirects, migrations, retired domains, archive candidates, visible date bounds, and current/prior/future-effective distinctions.

* REVIEW Execution Waves

** Wave 0: Identity, legal baseline, and fingerprint census

Load GEOIDs; verify state identity/domains; capture legal/retention/archive authorities; create unresolved county/component candidates; collect bounded platform/delegation/service/court/health fingerprints; record minimal history.

A reviewed passed Wave 0 gate binds exact subject/review revisions and variation evidence. No county is complete.

** Wave 1: State systems

Profile the state seeds and produce county-indexed crosswalks where exposed.

A reviewed passed Wave 1 gate requires reviewed profile/deployment revisions or reviewed blocked/unsupported subjects; adapter manifest/runtime bindings; rate/pagination/file/browser/auth/scope/artifact policy; raw evidence; Checkbook non-completeness; and minimal history.

** Wave 2: Franklin County

Franklin GEOID =39049= is the first complete county.

The jurisdiction gate binds:

- all sixteen exact route assessments and review heads
- all required subject review revisions
- reviewed applicability contract, policy, manifest set, and runtime set
- exact profile, deployment, search-plan, subfamily, gap, evidence, and artifact revisions
- canonical basis schema/algorithm, basis time/hash, freshness deadline, and valid-through

It passes only with reviewed required subfamilies, no nonterminal states, evidence-bearing not-applicable results, completed reviewed negative search/gap chains, fenced adapter attempts, deterministic basis replay, zero unexplained truncation, and zero unresolved required manual review.

Independent Review separately reviews the gate. Only a currently valid reviewed passed gate counts as Franklin completion.

** Wave 3: Fingerprint-driven diverse counties

Initial candidates: Cuyahoga =39035=, Hamilton =39061=, Lucas =39095=, Delaware =39041=, Athens =39009=, Vinton =39163=, Hancock =39063=.

Selection maximizes platform/vendor, hosting authority, publication maturity, office fragmentation, shared services, court/health topology, geography/size, and archive/migration variation. It retains a mid-sized non-large-metro or western/northwestern agricultural case unless equivalent coverage is proved.

Every county requires a reviewed valid jurisdiction gate. A separate reviewed Wave 3 gate binds county gates, selection evidence, and metrics.

Wave 3 requires no schema fork; at least 70% reusable-platform coverage of supported successful endpoints; reusable one-off adapter semantics/fixtures; deterministic replay; zero unexplained truncation/stale lease acceptance; reconciled deployments; review/unresolved-route metrics within a predeclared policy ceiling; and current negatives.

** Wave 4: All 88 counties

Use at most eight active county runs with versioned batch policy and host/vendor/deployment/tenant/key/credential/browser leases.

Each county requires a reviewed currently valid jurisdiction gate. State completion requires a reviewed state gate binding all 88 current county gates under one contract/policy snapshot.

** Wave 5: Historical expansion and reconciliation

Expand archives, reconcile migrations, establish refresh/retirement schedules, define tombstones/replacements, reconcile prior/current captures, and acquire bounded history.

Changes create new subject/review/gate revisions; prior gates remain replayable.

* REVIEW Courts and Negative Freshness

Court coverage binds county courts, clerks, and authoritative linked local-court assessments. No unified API is not not-applicable.

| Class | Maximum default age | Typical routes |
|-------+---------------------+----------------|
| high | 30 days | feeds, meetings, procurement, permits, operational metadata |
| medium | 90 days | open data, finance, elections, safety, public records |
| low | 180 days | identity, GIS, property, courts, archives |
| legal-versioned | next effective date or 180 days | law/legal policy |

Current completion becomes false immediately after gate =valid-through= or bound review invalidation; it does not wait for an expiry write.

* REVIEW Acceptance Criteria

1. All 88 counties are in scope.
2. All routes use one reviewed applicability contract.
3. Subject, review, and gate writers are disjoint.
4. Reviews are CAS-linear.
5. Route decisions pin exact reviewed subjects.
6. County/wave/state completion uses canonical time-bounded gates.
7. Historical identity is captured in every wave.
8. Checkbook does not imply finance completeness.
9. Court results bind linked assessments.
10. Adapter success cannot hide truncation, stale leases, or missing artifacts.
11. Envelope protocol and payload schema are distinct.
12. =PIPELINE-004= precedes implementation.
