* Dataset Rule

This page remains inside the single =wef= dataset.

All people, firms, political committees, government bodies, and defense companies discovered by this investigation are represented as nodes or sections inside this dataset. They are not split into separate person-, Trump-, Palantir-, or donor-specific datasets.

* Recursion Rule

A Depth 1 node is included only when it creates at least two independently documented paths back to the Depth 0 seed graph.

Relationship classes remain distinct:

- employment;
- company founding or governance;
- political donation or fundraising;
- government appointment;
- WEF program membership or event participation;
- investment ownership;
- commercial partnership;
- policy-advisory membership.

Co-attendance alone is not treated as proof of coordination.

* Findings

** Michael Kratsios

Michael Kratsios is the clearest person-level crossover between the WEF network, Peter Thiel's investment network, and both Trump administrations.

Documented edges:

1. The archived Trump White House biography states that Kratsios was a Principal at Thiel Capital before entering government.
2. The same biography identifies him as United States Chief Technology Officer and a technology-policy adviser to President Trump.
3. WEF included Kratsios in its 2020 class of Young Global Leaders.
4. The current White House identifies Kratsios as Director of the Office of Science and Technology Policy and President Trump's chief science and technology adviser.
5. WEF currently maintains a profile for Kratsios in that government role.
6. In March 2026, Trump appointed Kratsios and David Sacks as co-chairs of the President's Council of Advisors on Science and Technology.

Verified path:

#+begin_example
Peter Thiel
  `-- Thiel Capital principal --> Michael Kratsios
                                  |-- WEF Young Global Leader
                                  |-- first Trump administration CTO
                                  `-- second Trump administration OSTP director
#+end_example

Assessment: Kratsios is not merely adjacent to the network. His career directly traverses a Thiel-controlled investment organization, a formal WEF leadership program, and senior Trump technology-policy authority.

** David Sacks

David Sacks creates a direct Vance-Trump-WEF policy and fundraising path.

Documented edges:

1. Reuters reported that Vance introduced Donald Trump Jr. to Sacks and helped organize the June 2024 Trump fundraiser at Sacks's San Francisco home.
2. Reuters reported that the event raised approximately $12 million and that Sacks credited Vance with initiating it.
3. The Trump White House identifies Sacks as its AI and crypto czar.
4. Trump appointed Sacks as co-chair of PCAST with Kratsios in March 2026.
5. WEF reported that Sacks accompanied Trump's official delegation to Davos in January 2026.

Verified path:

#+begin_example
JD Vance -- organized Trump fundraiser --> David Sacks
                                              |-- Trump AI and crypto adviser
                                              |-- PCAST co-chair with Kratsios
                                              `-- Trump Davos delegation / WEF
#+end_example

Assessment: Sacks is the strongest direct bridge from Vance's Silicon Valley fundraising operation into a current Trump policy role and then into an official WEF event delegation.

** Jacob Helberg

Jacob Helberg connects Palantir's executive orbit to Trump political financing and then to a senior State Department appointment.

Documented edges:

1. Reuters identified Helberg as an adviser to Palantir and reported that he donated approximately $1 million to Trump's 2024 campaign while also helping raise money.
2. Helberg attended the June 2024 Sacks fundraiser where Vance acted as an organizer and liaison.
3. Congress records show that the Senate confirmed Helberg on 7 October 2025 as Under Secretary of State for Economic Growth, Energy, and the Environment.
4. Reuters reported Helberg acting in 2026 as a senior State Department official on United States-China and transatlantic technology policy.

Verified path:

#+begin_example
Palantir / Alex Karp orbit -- adviser --> Jacob Helberg
                                            |-- Trump donor and fundraiser
                                            `-- Trump State Department under secretary
#+end_example

Assessment: Helberg has no verified direct WEF role in the current source packet. His importance is the movement from Palantir-adjacent technology policy into Trump fundraising and then formal state authority.

** Trae Stephens

Trae Stephens connects Palantir, Peter Thiel's Founders Fund, Donald Trump's Defense Department transition operation, and Anduril.

Documented edges:

1. Founders Fund identifies Stephens as a partner, a former early Palantir employee, and co-founder and executive chairman of Anduril.
2. Bloomberg reported in November 2016 that Stephens joined Trump's Defense Department transition team to help shape policy and vet personnel.
3. Palantir's 2026 proxy reports a commercial relationship with Anduril, payments of $17.66 million from Anduril during 2025, and a $40 million Palantir investment in Anduril's Series G financing.
4. The proxy states that Peter Thiel may be deemed to hold more than 10 percent of Anduril through related entities.
5. Palantir identifies Anduril as part of its TITAN defense-program team.

Verified path:

#+begin_example
Palantir -- early employee --> Trae Stephens -- partner --> Founders Fund / Thiel
                                  |                         |
                                  |                         `-- investment network
                                  |-- Trump DoD transition
                                  `-- co-founder/chair --> Anduril
                                                           |-- Palantir customer
                                                           |-- Palantir investment
                                                           `-- joint TITAN team
#+end_example

Assessment: Stephens is the strongest bridge from Palantir personnel into the defense-startup layer and Trump's Defense Department staffing process.

** Joe Lonsdale

Joe Lonsdale connects Palantir's founding group to pro-Trump political financing and later Vance fundraising.

Documented edges:

1. 8VC identifies Lonsdale as Palantir's 2003 co-founder and as founder and managing partner of 8VC.
2. Federal-election reporting cited by Reuters identified Lonsdale Enterprises as a contributor to America PAC, the pro-Trump political committee.
3. Reporting identified a $1 million contribution from Lonsdale to America PAC in 2024.
4. Axios reported that Lonsdale hosted a high-dollar RNC fundraiser headlined by Vice President Vance in March 2026.

Verified path:

#+begin_example
Palantir -- co-founder --> Joe Lonsdale -- America PAC funding --> Trump
                              `-- hosted RNC fundraiser --> JD Vance
#+end_example

Assessment: Lonsdale is a direct founder-level Palantir bridge into both Trump campaign infrastructure and Vance's continuing national donor operation.

** Rockbridge Network

Rockbridge is an institutional amplifier of the Vance-Thiel political network.

Documented edges:

1. Reuters reported that Vance co-founded Rockbridge in 2019.
2. The same investigation identified Peter Thiel and Rebekah Mercer among its early financial backers.
3. Rockbridge officials also appeared in Vance's fundraising operation.
4. The network financed or coordinated media, polling, voter-turnout, and policy organizations aligned with Trump-style nationalism.

Verified path:

#+begin_example
JD Vance -- co-founder --> Rockbridge Network <-- early financial backing -- Peter Thiel
                              `-- staff and donor overlap --> Vance / Trump political operation
#+end_example

Assessment: Rockbridge is not a WEF body and no direct WEF relationship is established. It matters as the structured political infrastructure surrounding the Vance-Thiel branch.

** Anduril Industries

Anduril is an organizational bridge rather than merely a company associated with one former Palantir employee.

Documented edges:

1. Stephens is Anduril's co-founder and executive chairman.
2. Peter Thiel may be deemed to hold more than 10 percent through related entities, according to Palantir's proxy.
3. Palantir received $17.66 million from Anduril in 2025.
4. Palantir invested $40 million in Anduril's Series G financing in 2025.
5. Palantir and Anduril participate together in the United States Army TITAN program team.

Assessment: These are personnel, ownership, investment, customer, and defense-program edges. They demonstrate an integrated commercial and capital relationship but do not make the companies one entity.

** President's Council of Advisors on Science and Technology

PCAST is a current policy-convergence venue created by Trump.

Documented edges:

1. Trump appointed David Sacks and Michael Kratsios as co-chairs in March 2026.
2. Initial members include Marc Andreessen, Sergey Brin, Safra Catz, Michael Dell, Fred Ehrsam, Larry Ellison, Jensen Huang, Lisa Su, and Mark Zuckerberg.
3. Kratsios connects PCAST to Thiel Capital and WEF's Young Global Leaders.
4. Sacks connects PCAST to Vance's Trump fundraising operation and the 2026 Davos delegation.

Assessment: PCAST is a formal government advisory body. It is not a WEF body, but its leadership joins two independently verified cross-network actors.

* Analytical Conclusions

1. The next layer is principally a personnel pipeline, not a WEF-membership chain.
2. Kratsios is the most explicit WEF-Thiel-Trump crossover because all three relationships are formally documented.
3. Sacks is the strongest current bridge joining Vance's fundraising network, Trump policy authority, and a Trump WEF delegation.
4. Palantir alumni and founders repeatedly appear in Trump transition, fundraising, diplomatic, and defense-technology roles.
5. Anduril is tied to Palantir through personnel, Thiel-related ownership, commercial payments, direct investment, and a joint Army program.
6. The Vance network operates through multiple vehicles: individual donors, hosted fundraisers, Rockbridge, America PAC, and formal Republican Party fundraising.
7. WEF remains concentrated on the Fink-Karp-Kratsios-Sacks side of the graph; no direct WEF role has been established for Helberg, Stephens, Lonsdale, Rockbridge, or Anduril.

* Claims Not Established

- that WEF selected or directed Trump administration appointments;
- that Young Global Leader status creates an employment or command obligation;
- that Palantir directed the political activity of former employees or founders;
- that Anduril and Palantir are under common operational control;
- that a political donation proves control over a recipient;
- that PCAST is subordinate to WEF;
- that every member of PCAST belongs to the same political faction.
