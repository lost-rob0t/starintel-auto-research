* REVIEW Protocol Alignment Resolution

The Ohio review exposed protocol-v1 gaps in =PIPELINE-003=. Those gaps are now formalized in =PIPELINE-004= rather than left as implicit Ohio exceptions.

The amendment requires:

- exact profile, deployment, search-plan, decision, gap, assessment, review, and coverage-gate revision IDs
- disjoint subject authorities and Independent Review Authority
- Route Assessment and Coverage Gate authorities
- atomic reviewed completion gates with basis hashes
- applicability-contract and policy-snapshot bindings
- revision-aware events, results, checkpoints, routing, and scheduling
- durable job and lease-bound attempt separation with fencing tokens

The amendment must be approved and reconciled with =PIPELINE-003= before Ohio implementation.
