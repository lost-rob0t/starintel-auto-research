* Research Question

Should duplicate or near-duplicate observations be retained as corroborating evidence even when duplicate payload storage is eliminated?

* Finding

*KEEP WITH SOURCE-INDEPENDENCE CHECKS.* Knowledge-fusion research treats repeated observations from multiple sources and extractors as evidence to be combined while accounting for source reliability and extraction error. This supports separating physical deduplication from semantic evidence counting.

The critical caveat is dependence. Ten pages that copied one press release are not ten independent confirmations. StarIntel should preserve lineage sufficient to distinguish independent observations from syndicated, mirrored, quoted, scraped, or recursively copied material.

* Research Gaps

- Define source-independence and shared-lineage heuristics.
- Decide how near-duplicate text maps to claim-level corroboration.
- Separate confidence gained from independent sources from confidence gained from repeated extraction of one source.

* Footnotes and Glossary

- *Corroboration*: Multiple observations supporting the same claim.
- *Source dependence*: Evidence items sharing an upstream origin and therefore not representing independent confirmation.
