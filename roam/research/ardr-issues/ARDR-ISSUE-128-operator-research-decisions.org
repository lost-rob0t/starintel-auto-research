* Durable Decisions

The operator approved the Titanic/ZeroMQ direction subject to a mandatory broader StarRouter/transport/API redraft, and approved STAR-RESEARCH-010, 013, 014, 015, and 016 for promotion into design under the governance active when the issue was written. STAR-RESEARCH-001 OSINT Feature Landscape was rejected for design promotion pending later reopening.

The mandatory transport redraft treats ZeroMQ as a first-class transport where semantics permit, makes StarRouter the transport-neutral routing/control seam, requires Common Lisp gserver integration, preserves HTTP/RabbitMQ as optional adapters where useful, and requires transport-equivalent observable semantics to preserve durability, idempotency, fencing, authorization, provenance, cancellation, deadlines, overload, reconnect/recovery, and failure reporting.
