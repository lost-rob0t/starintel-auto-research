* REVIEW Objective

Define one durable national-compatible contract for Ohio state systems, county systems, components, delegated vendor portals, shared deployments, static files, APIs, dashboards, form-driven searches, document archives, applicability decisions, and explicit coverage gaps without creating a schema fork per county.

* REVIEW Invariants

1. A search result is never a verified source profile.
2. Every verified profile revision links to official ownership or delegation evidence.
3. Every profile revision is scoped to one government unit or component and one source family.
4. Shared vendor deployments are represented separately from jurisdiction-specific authority and coverage.
5. One endpoint may support multiple profiles when authority or coverage differs.
6. One profile may contain multiple locators when they are one logical source.
7. Raw evidence and retrieval events are immutable.
8. Profile revisions are append-only and explicitly supersede prior revisions.
9. Route-assessment revisions are append-only and bind exact profile revisions.
10. A source profile does not imply complete route coverage.
11. Negative findings are evidence-bearing, expiring assessments.
12. Review state is separate from source or acquisition state.
13. Current, historical, and future-effective content are measured separately.
14. Personal-data fields and access restrictions are recorded before acquisition is authorized.
15. =:not-applicable= requires positive authority or service-topology evidence, not failure to find a source.
16. A later source revision never changes the evidence basis of an older route assessment.

* REVIEW Official Status

=:official-status= is one of:

- =:verified-official=
- =:verified-delegated=
- =:candidate-official=
- =:candidate-delegated=
- =:unverified=
- =:rejected=
- =:retired=

Promotion to =:verified-official= requires evidence from a government-controlled domain, authoritative registry, or authenticated official publication.

Promotion to =:verified-delegated= requires an official government page, document, or authority record linking the vendor system to the government unit or component.

A matching logo, organization name, TLS certificate, or search result is insufficient by itself.

** Initial Ohio county subfamily examples

| Route | Required or conditional subfamilies |
|-------+-------------------------------------|
| identity | county identity, elected components, service-provider topology |
| meetings | commissioners; other boards when the applicability predicate is true |
| finance | budget, audit; payroll, checkbook, debt, grants when published or legally maintained by the unit |
| property | auditor/assessment, recorder/deeds, treasurer/tax, parcel/GIS or resolved service provider |
| courts | county courts and clerks plus authoritative links to local courts serving the county |
| elections | board of elections, local results; campaign finance when maintained locally |
| public safety | sheriff; jail and incident/enforcement systems when operated by the county |
| permits | building, zoning, planning, health, and code subfamilies according to service topology |

The complete initial contract must enumerate all sixteen routes before Franklin County begins.

* REVIEW Route Assessment Revision

Each government unit receives append-only route-assessment revisions.

#+begin_src lisp

#+end_src

=assessment-id= is the stable logical identity. =assessment-revision-id= is the immutable decision revision. Reassessment, changed evidence, contract migration, or expiry creates a new revision and preserves the old one.

A route assessment cannot reference only logical profile IDs. It pins exact profile revisions, search-plan revisions, evidence records, gaps, and the applicability-contract version.

Route states are:

- =:unassessed=
- =:candidate-found=
- =:verified=
- =:verified-partial=
- =:not-published=
- =:records-request-required=
- =:authentication-required=
- =:paywalled=
- =:blocked=
- =:retired=
- =:ambiguous=
- =:not-applicable=

=:verified= requires every required subfamily to have a reviewed =:verified= decision or a contract-permitted reviewed =:not-applicable= decision.

=:verified-partial= is allowed only when the applicability rule permits it, the covered and missing scope is explicit, and every missing required portion has a gap and reassessment policy.

=:candidate-found=, =:ambiguous=, and =:unassessed= are nonterminal and cannot satisfy a pilot or county completion gate.

* REVIEW Independent State Models

Source state and review state are independent fields with separate authorities and transitions.

** Source state

Allowed source states:

- =:candidate=
- =:observed=
- =:active=
- =:changed=
- =:blocked=
- =:retired=
- =:rejected=

#+begin_example
:candidate -> :observed -> :active
:active -> :changed -> :active
:active -> :blocked -> :active
:active -> :retired
:candidate -> :rejected
#+end_example

Adapter workers may propose =:candidate= or =:observed=. The Source Profile Authority alone writes canonical source-state transitions after validation. Retirement and reactivation require successor or current-observation evidence.

** Review state

Allowed review states:

- =:unreviewed=
- =:evidence-collected=
- =:verification-pending=
- =:reviewed=
- =:changes-requested=
- =:invalidated=

#+begin_example
:unreviewed -> :evidence-collected -> :verification-pending -> :reviewed
:verification-pending -> :changes-requested -> :evidence-collected
:reviewed -> :invalidated -> :evidence-collected -> :verification-pending -> :reviewed
#+end_example

Workers may collect evidence but cannot mark a canonical revision reviewed. The independent review authority performs =:reviewed=, =:changes-requested=, and =:invalidated= transitions.

A source transition to =:changed= creates a new profile revision with review state =:invalidated= or =:unreviewed=. The previously reviewed revision remains immutable and queryable, but the changed revision cannot satisfy a current route gate until reviewed.

=:retired= is a source state, not a review state. A retired revision still requires reviewed retirement evidence before it can satisfy a =:retired= route result.

* REVIEW Validation Rules

A profile revision is invalid when:

- the government unit or component cannot be resolved
- the route or data family is missing
- no locator exists
- verified status lacks authority evidence
- delegated status lacks an official delegation link
- deployment references do not resolve to an immutable revision
- coverage scope is absent for shared or participating-unit sources
- preferred adapter lacks the required capability
- temporal coverage is asserted without evidence
- a partial source is marked complete
- browser evidence lacks a replayable capture bundle
- OCR-derived text lacks source-image and confidence references
- authentication requirements are omitted after observation
- promoted evidence lacks content hashes or retrieval events
- review fields are self-approved by the proposing worker

A route-assessment revision is invalid when:

- the applicability contract or version is missing
- a required subfamily has no decision
- a =:not-applicable= decision lacks contract-permitted authority evidence
- it references logical profile IDs without revision IDs
- a referenced profile revision is not reviewed
- a negative terminal state lacks a completed search-plan revision, gap, and =reassess-after=
- a stale negative result is used for current completion
- =:verified-partial= lacks explicit missing scope and gaps
- =:candidate-found=, =:ambiguous=, or =:unassessed= is used to pass a pilot gate
- a new decision mutates an old assessment instead of superseding it

* REVIEW Versioning and Migration

- logical profile IDs remain stable across revisions
- profile, deployment, search-plan, and assessment revision IDs are immutable
- every superseding revision points to its predecessor
- locator IDs remain stable while the logical endpoint remains the same
- vendor migrations create new locators and successor relationships
- retired profiles and assessments remain queryable
- applicability-contract migrations force new route-assessment revisions
- unknown fields from a newer contract are preserved when possible and never silently discarded
- canonical latest-state queries never erase the exact basis of historical decisions

* REVIEW Acceptance Criteria

1. one schema describes state, county, component, and shared-deployment sources
2. verified profile revisions always have authority evidence
3. route coverage and profile lifecycle remain separate
4. route assessments pin exact immutable profile and search-plan revisions
5. route assessments are append-only and explicitly superseding
6. the applicability contract deterministically defines required subfamilies
7. =:not-applicable= requires positive authority or topology evidence
8. negative findings expire under a declared freshness class
9. source state and review state have independent enums, authorities, and transitions
10. changed sources reopen review without mutating prior revisions
11. temporal and jurisdictional completeness are explicit
12. partial current-year or participating-unit sources cannot be marked complete
13. browser and OCR evidence have reproducible boundaries
14. proposals and canonical writes are separated
15. Ohio extensions do not fork the national schema
16. the contract supports =STAR-GOVDATA-DESIGN-003=

- verified official Ohio domain
- verified delegated county vendor portal
- rejected lookalike domain
- shared vendor deployment used by multiple jurisdictions
- source with API and browser interfaces and replayable browser bundle
- scanned PDF producing =:ocr-required=
