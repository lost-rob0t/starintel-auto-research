* Research Question

What directly documented relationships connect the World Economic Forum, Donald Trump, JD Vance, Larry Fink, BlackRock, Palantir, and Palantir's founders or executives?

This depth is deliberately restricted to direct edges between the seed entities and the first indispensable bridge nodes. It does not treat event co-attendance, investment ownership, political donation, employment, governance, or organizational partnership as interchangeable relationships.

* Dataset Decision

All depths of this investigation use the existing =auto-research= dataset.

No new WEF, Trump, Vance, BlackRock, Palantir, or person-specific dataset is created. Later recursion pages remain under =roam/research/auto-research/= and link back to this seed page.

* Seed Entities

- World Economic Forum, or WEF
- Donald J. Trump
- JD Vance
- Laurence D. Fink
- BlackRock
- Palantir Technologies

The first indispensable bridge nodes are:

- Peter Thiel
- Alexander Karp
- Stephen Cohen

* Direct Findings

** Donald Trump and the World Economic Forum

Donald Trump has repeatedly addressed the World Economic Forum while serving as President of the United States.

Verified appearances in the source packet include:

- an in-person address at Davos on 26 January 2018;
- a remote special address on 23 January 2025;
- an in-person special address on 21 January 2026.

The relationship is direct and recurring: WEF supplied the platform and Trump used it to present United States economic, trade, energy, immigration, and foreign-policy positions.

This evidence establishes access and repeated engagement. It does not establish WEF membership, ideological agreement, or command over Trump.

** Larry Fink and the World Economic Forum

WEF currently identifies Larry Fink as Co-Chair of its Board of Trustees. Fink is also Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of BlackRock.

This is a formal governance relationship, not merely conference attendance.

WEF states that its Board of Trustees oversees the Forum's work and acts as guardian of its mission and values. Fink therefore occupies a materially stronger position in the WEF network than an ordinary corporate participant.

** Palantir and the World Economic Forum

WEF lists Palantir Technologies among the partners of its 2026 Annual Meeting. Historical WEF partner listings also include Palantir.

Palantir CEO and co-founder Alex Karp has participated in multiple WEF sessions. On 20 January 2026, WEF published a session explicitly identifying Alex Karp and Larry Fink as its two speakers.

The verified direct path is:

#+begin_example
Larry Fink -- WEF co-chair and session speaker --> Alex Karp -- Palantir CEO
#+end_example

** Donald Trump and Larry Fink

President-elect Trump named Larry Fink to the President's Strategic and Policy Forum on 2 December 2016. The forum was designed to provide direct private-sector input to the President on economic growth, job creation, productivity, and government policy.

A later meeting transcript also records Fink participating in the forum with Trump.

This is a documented advisory relationship. It does not establish that Trump adopted Fink's recommendations or that Fink controlled administration policy.

** Donald Trump and JD Vance

The White House identifies JD Vance as Vice President of the United States under President Donald Trump.

This is the strongest formal political edge in the graph:

#+begin_example
Donald Trump -- President / selected running mate --> JD Vance -- Vice President
#+end_example

** JD Vance and Peter Thiel

The documented Vance-Thiel relationship has multiple independent components:

- Vance worked at Mithril Capital, a Thiel-linked venture-capital firm;
- Thiel became a major financial supporter of Vance's 2022 Senate campaign;
- Thiel is reported as a financial backer of the Vance-co-founded Rockbridge Network;
- the relationship supplied Vance with access to a wider technology, venture-capital, and donor network.

This is not a one-event association. It is an employment, patronage, financing, and network-access relationship.

** Peter Thiel and Palantir

Palantir's 2026 proxy identifies Peter Thiel as a co-founder and chairman of its board since 2003. The filing also identifies Alexander Karp, Stephen Cohen, and Peter Thiel as the founders participating in Palantir's founder voting structure.

The direct Vance-to-Palantir route is therefore:

#+begin_example
JD Vance -- employee, beneficiary, network ally --> Peter Thiel -- co-founder and chairman --> Palantir
#+end_example

** BlackRock and Palantir

Palantir's 2026 proxy lists BlackRock as a greater-than-five-percent stockholder with:

- 158,217,849 Class A shares;
- 6.9 percent of Palantir Class A shares;
- 3.5 percent of total voting power as calculated in the filing.

This is institutional beneficial ownership reported for BlackRock-managed holdings. It is not evidence that Larry Fink personally owns those shares.

The same filing reports that Palantir's founder structure held 49.999999 percent of voting power. BlackRock's position therefore does not establish corporate control over Palantir.

** JD Vance and WEF

No official WEF biography, Young Global Leader listing, trustee record, or official speaker record for JD Vance was identified in this depth's source search.

WEF pages that merely mention Vance, Trump, or =Hillbilly Elegy= are not treated as evidence of affiliation.

This is a bounded negative finding, not proof that Vance has never attended any event involving WEF participants.

* Conclusions

1. The evidence supports an overlapping network, not a single unified command structure.
2. Vance's strongest route into Palantir is Peter Thiel.
3. Fink's strongest routes are WEF governance, BlackRock leadership, Trump's former business advisory forum, and direct WEF dialogue with Karp.
4. Palantir is the central organizational crossover because it participates in the WEF partner environment while its chairman is Vance's most important documented technology-political patron.
5. BlackRock's Palantir holdings are material but do not overcome Palantir's founder voting structure.
6. No verified direct WEF affiliation for Vance was found at this depth.

* Claims Not Established

- WEF membership by Donald Trump or JD Vance
- WEF control over Trump, Vance, BlackRock, or Palantir
- Larry Fink's personal ownership of BlackRock-managed Palantir shares
- BlackRock control of Palantir
- coordination inferred solely from appearing at the same event
- a single legal or operational entity joining WEF, Trump, Vance, Fink, and Palantir
