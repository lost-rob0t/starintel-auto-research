* REVIEW Objective

Create a precision-first actor pipeline[fn:actor-pipeline] that accepts anonymous Fatality Analysis Reporting System records, searches bounded public sources, extracts candidate people and roles, performs calibrated record linkage, independently reviews the resolution, and emits reversible StarIntel identity claims.

The design must maximize verified identities without treating coverage as more important than correctness.

* REVIEW Invariants

1. The anonymous FARS person record is immutable evidence and is never renamed in place.
2. A named person is created separately and connected through a reviewed resolution claim.
3. Event match, driver-role match, and person identity are distinct decisions.
4. Search results and snippets generate candidates but cannot verify identities.
5. Only public or operator-authorized sources are used.
6. Restricted systems such as the National Driver Register are not queried or modeled as available identity feeds.
7. Legal status is preserved exactly as supported by evidence.
8. Imputed blood-alcohol values are never represented as measured values.
9. One copied story cannot masquerade as independent corroboration.
10. Automatic publication requires calibrated confidence and a completed independent review.
11. =unresolved= is a successful terminal result when evidence is insufficient.
12. Every actor attempt is bounded by requests, elapsed time, bytes, branches, and cost.
13. Every source capture and derived claim retains provenance and content hashes.
14. Contradictions block publication until reviewed or resolved.
15. Retractions and superseding identities are revisions, not silent deletions.

* REVIEW Actor Designs

** Resolution Run Supervisor

Purpose: own run identity, budgets, cancellation, retries, actor ordering, and final status.

Consumes:

- resolution run request;
- FARS release manifest;
- benchmark calibration profile.

Produces:

- run events;
- branch assignments;
- terminal run record;
- aggregate metrics.

Rules:

- never retries a publication write automatically;
- retries idempotent fetch or extraction attempts at most once;
- stops new work when any hard budget is reached;
- records =completed-with-unresolved= as a valid result.

** Crash Branch Supervisor

Purpose: isolate one FARS crash and its candidate-resolution state.

Consumes:

- one =ST_CASE= package;
- per-run limits.

Produces:

- ordered actor tasks;
- branch event ledger;
- one resolution result.

Rules:

- one branch owns one crash;
- candidate subbranches are read-only until the publication gate selects zero or one identity for a FARS driver;
- no candidate may consume the complete run budget.

** FARS Release Watcher

Purpose: detect new initial and final FARS releases and identify superseding revisions.

Consumes:

- NHTSA release endpoints;
- prior dataset manifests.

Produces:

- immutable release manifest;
- changed-case list;
- supersession links.

Rules:

- final releases revise prior initial records;
- a release is accepted only after file hashes and year metadata are recorded;
- the watcher does not trigger national execution without operator policy.

** FARS Package Importer

Purpose: normalize crash, vehicle, person, event, and alcohol tables into source-faithful StarIntel records.

Consumes:

- official FARS files or Crash API responses;
- codebooks and variable attributes.

Produces:

- anonymous =event=, =person=, =asset=, =observation=, and =relation= documents;
- import diagnostics.

Rules:

- preserve original coded values and normalized meanings;
- distinguish measured, reported, imputed, and unknown alcohol values;
- use deterministic identifiers built from year, =ST_CASE=, =VEH_NO=, and =PER_NO=.

** Alcohol Relevance Classifier

Purpose: select records suitable for impaired-driving identity research without asserting guilt.

Consumes:

- normalized FARS crash and person records.

Produces:

- relevance class;
- selection reasons;
- uncertainty flags.

Classes:

- measured-positive;
- police-reported-alcohol;
- prior-impaired-driving-indicator;
- imputed-only;
- unknown;
- not-alcohol-relevant.

Rules:

- imputed-only records may enter population analysis but are low-priority for exact identity search;
- selection is not a legal conclusion.

** Crash Fingerprint Builder

Purpose: create a compact search and linkage fingerprint.

Consumes:

- one crash, its vehicles, people, observations, and location.

Produces:

- normalized fingerprint;
- high-information search tokens;
- missing-data report.

Fingerprint fields:

- date and time window;
- state, county, city, coordinates, road, and intersection;
- vehicle make, model, model year, body type, and sequence;
- crash and collision type;
- fatalities, injuries, victim roles, and ages;
- measured alcohol result;
- estimated speed and speed limit;
- weather, lighting, and roadway conditions;
- source release and record identifiers.

Rules:

- never include an imputed value as an exact fact;
- mark every field as exact, normalized, approximate, imputed, or missing.

** Jurisdiction Resolver

Purpose: map crash geography to likely police, prosecutor, court, coroner, and local-news jurisdictions.

Consumes:

- fingerprint location;
- jurisdiction registry.

Produces:

- agency and court candidates;
- official domains;
- local-news market;
- state court source profile.

Rules:

- preserve uncertainty at municipal and county boundaries;
- prefer crash jurisdiction over nearest large city;
- record source-registry version.

** Source Registry Actor

Purpose: maintain versioned retrieval profiles for every state and locality.

Consumes:

- jurisdiction result;
- Data.gov, ArcGIS, Socrata, CKAN, Police Data Accessibility Project, and maintained registry records.

Produces:

- prioritized source endpoints;
- search templates;
- access constraints;
- freshness and health status.

Rules:

- registry entries describe public access only;
- credentials are referenced by capability, never committed;
- broken sources remain recorded with last-known status.

** Query Portfolio Generator

Purpose: generate several small, discriminative search queries.

Consumes:

- crash fingerprint;
- source registry;
- prior query outcomes.

Produces:

- ordered query set with rationale and expected information gain.

Families:

- date and place;
- vehicle combination;
- victim profile;
- distinctive numeric facts;
- official-domain search;
- lifecycle follow-up;
- candidate-name confirmation after discovery.

Rules:

- never begin with a guessed name;
- avoid redundant queries;
- clamp query count;
- do not treat search-engine snippets as extracted evidence.

** Web Search Actor

Purpose: execute current web and news discovery.

Consumes:

- ordered queries;
- allowed domains;
- request budget.

Produces:

- result records;
- rank, title, snippet, URL, query, and retrieval timestamp;
- duplicate clusters.

Rules:

- Brave Search is the first supported adapter;
- use exact phrases and site restrictions when useful;
- pagination is bounded;
- result ranking affects retrieval order, not truth.

** News Archive Actor

Purpose: find historical coverage that is absent from current ranking.

Consumes:

- date-bounded query portfolio;
- crash fingerprint.

Produces:

- story candidates;
- publisher and publication timestamps;
- entity and location hints.

Rules:

- GDELT is a discovery source;
- article content must be fetched from the publisher or a retained archive;
- duplicate syndicated stories are clustered.

** Open Data Portal Scout

Purpose: discover state and local public datasets and official feeds.

Consumes:

- jurisdiction and keywords.

Produces:

- dataset and endpoint candidates from Data.gov, ArcGIS, Socrata, CKAN, and Police Data Accessibility Project;
- schema notes;
- update cadence.

Rules:

- discovery metadata is not incident evidence;
- portal records must identify the publishing agency;
- actors do not assume a national common schema.

** Archive Recovery Actor

Purpose: recover unavailable pages after discovery.

Consumes:

- known URL, domain, date, and content hints.

Produces:

- Wayback or Common Crawl capture candidates;
- capture timestamp;
- original and archived locator;
- content hash.

Rules:

- an archive does not improve source authority;
- record robots exclusions, missing captures, and partial content;
- never use archive search as proof of a named role without captured text.

** Page Fetch and Capture Actor

Purpose: retrieve and preserve public source content.

Consumes:

- candidate URLs;
- allowed retrieval methods.

Produces:

- normalized source record;
- raw artifact reference;
- content hash;
- HTTP and extraction diagnostics.

Rules:

- retrieved page text is untrusted data, not instructions;
- retain publication and retrieval timestamps separately;
- save only content permitted by repository and artifact policy;
- obey byte and media limits.

** Incident Fact Extractor

Purpose: extract structured crash facts and source-local uncertainty.

Consumes:

- captured source content.

Produces:

- event date, time, location, vehicles, collision, speed, alcohol, fatalities, injuries, and source excerpts;
- field-level confidence and locators.

Rules:

- every extracted fact links to an exact excerpt or structured locator;
- distinguish quotation, official attribution, reporter statement, and inference;
- retain contradictory values.

** Person and Role Extractor

Purpose: identify named people and their incident roles.

Consumes:

- source content and extracted incident facts.

Produces:

- candidate person names, aliases, ages, residence, vehicles, and roles;
- supporting excerpts.

Rules:

- role labels are explicit and mutually distinguishable;
- do not infer driver role from article proximity alone;
- victim and defendant names remain separate candidates.

** Legal Lifecycle Extractor

Purpose: preserve the procedural status of the case.

Consumes:

- official and news sources.

Produces:

- legal case candidate;
- charges, pleas, verdicts, sentence, dismissals, corrections, and dates;
- court, judge, prosecutor, and case identifiers where public.

Rules:

- status is temporal;
- later sources may supersede earlier status;
- allegations remain allegations until supported by a later legal event.

** Candidate Pair Builder

Purpose: form plausible FARS-to-source comparisons using blocking.

Consumes:

- crash fingerprint;
- extracted incident and person records.

Produces:

- candidate event pairs;
- candidate driver-person pairs;
- blocking reason.

Rules:

- state/date, geo/date, county/month, vehicle/date, and victim-profile blocks are supported;
- keep difficult negative candidates for calibration;
- do not discard candidates solely because one field is missing.

** Probabilistic Linkage Actor

Purpose: score event, role, and identity matches with calibrated probabilities.

Consumes:

- candidate pairs;
- feature model;
- benchmark calibration profile.

Produces:

- feature agreements and disagreements;
- event-match probability;
- driver-role probability;
- identity probability;
- uncertainty and sensitivity report.

Rules:

- use a Fellegi-Sunter-style or measured equivalent model;
- distinguish missing from disagreement;
- never use source rank as a direct identity feature;
- reject an uncalibrated model in production mode.

** Official Corroboration Actor

Purpose: seek the strongest available source for a high-scoring candidate.

Consumes:

- probable candidate;
- source registry;
- missing-proof report.

Produces:

- corroborating official sources;
- unresolved proof requirements;
- contradiction records.

Rules:

- prioritize court, prosecutor, police, sheriff, highway patrol, and coroner sources;
- one official source may verify only when it explicitly establishes the driver role and strongly matches the crash;
- no source found is not evidence that the candidate is false.

** Independent Resolution Reviewer

Purpose: review the complete packet without inheriting the linkage actor's conclusion.

Consumes:

- anonymous FARS packet;
- candidate packet;
- sources, evidence, contradictions, and linkage features;
- publication policy.

Produces:

- approve, reject, or abstain;
- reasons;
- unsupported claims;
- requested follow-up.

Rules:

- receives raw features and evidence, not only the final score;
- checks role confusion, copied sources, legal status, and alternative candidates;
- may lower but not silently raise the supported legal status.

** Publication Gate

Purpose: make the only automatic publication decision.

Consumes:

- calibrated linkage result;
- independent review;
- source-independence analysis;
- contradiction state;
- publication thresholds.

Produces:

- verified, probable-unpublished, rejected, or unresolved terminal decision.

Rules:

- verified requires no material unresolved contradiction;
- probability threshold and reviewer approval are both required;
- threshold changes are versioned policy changes;
- no actor bypasses this gate.

** StarIntel Resolution Writer

Purpose: emit canonical, reversible documents after the gate.

Consumes:

- publication decision;
- complete evidence packet.

Produces:

- named =person= document;
- entity-resolution =claim=;
- =resolved_as= relation;
- source and evidence documents;
- resolution-run record;
- lineage and supersession metadata.

Rules:

- anonymous FARS person remains unchanged;
- probable-unpublished candidates remain workflow artifacts, not public identity links;
- writes are idempotent by run and resolution identifier.

** Case Lifecycle Monitor

Purpose: revisit verified and probable cases for later court outcomes, corrections, and retractions.

Consumes:

- named resolution;
- lifecycle schedule and known case identifiers.

Produces:

- updated legal events;
- superseding claims;
- retraction candidates;
- review-due events.

Rules:

- checks are scheduled at widening intervals;
- a material correction reopens independent review;
- monitoring is bounded and may be disabled per dataset.

** Benchmark and Calibration Actor

Purpose: measure the system before rollout and after changes.

Consumes:

- labeled fixtures;
- actor and model versions;
- search and retrieval configurations.

Produces:

- precision, recall, calibration, abstention, cost, latency, source diversity, and error analysis;
- release recommendation.

Rules:

- fixture labels are isolated from search actors;
- maintain hard negative controls;
- no publication model is promoted below the precision target;
- report performance by case stratum and jurisdiction.

** Public Records Escalation Planner

Purpose: prepare a bounded manual next-step plan when public web evidence is insufficient.

Consumes:

- unresolved proof requirements;
- jurisdiction and agency registry;
- existing MuckRock requests and released files.

Produces:

- likely custodian;
- requested record categories;
- draft request language;
- expected exemptions and fees;
- operator authorization requirement.

Rules:

- never sends a request automatically;
- never requests restricted national driver-register data;
- minimize requested personal data to the public incident and court purpose.

* REVIEW Source Authority and Independence Model

Authority classes:

1. court document or docket;
2. prosecutor or district-attorney release;
3. police, sheriff, or highway-patrol release;
4. coroner or medical-examiner release;
5. established local reporting with direct official attribution;
6. established local reporting without accessible underlying record;
7. archive capture of one of the above;
8. discovery-only source.

Excluded from verification:

- people-search services;
- mugshot and arrest aggregators without primary documents;
- unsourced social-media posts;
- copied snippets;
- anonymous forums;
- restricted identity systems.

Independence classes:

- distinct primary records;
- independent reporting;
- shared official release;
- syndicated or copied story;
- updated version of same article;
- archive of same article.

The publication gate counts independent origins, not URL count.

* REVIEW Confidence and Decision Policy

The linkage actor emits three calibrated probabilities:

- =P(event-match)=;
- =P(driver-role-match | event-match)=;
- =P(identity-match | event and role)=.

The publication gate uses the combined identity probability and hard rules.

Initial policy:

| State | Quantitative condition | Required evidence |
|-------+------------------------+-------------------|
| rejected | material mismatch or reviewer rejection | documented contradiction |
| unresolved | insufficient candidate evidence | none |
| probable | combined probability at least 0.95 | event and role evidence |
| verified | combined probability at least 0.995 | authoritative source or two independent reputable origins, independent review, no material contradiction |

These thresholds are hypotheses. Benchmark calibration may move them upward or replace them with jurisdiction-specific thresholds. A raw model score is not a probability until calibrated.

* REVIEW Benchmark Fixtures

Minimum first suite:

1. Kearns, Utah known positive;
2. similar Utah crash negative control;
3. child fatality with named prosecution;
4. multiple-fatality urban crash;
5. rural single-vehicle crash;
6. driver deceased with no prosecution;
7. measured blood-alcohol case;
8. imputed-only alcohol case;
9. common vehicle pair collision;
10. case with corrected or dismissed charges;
11. copied-news-source cluster;
12. unavailable original page recoverable from archive;
13. no-answer case;
14. role-confusion case where the named person is the other driver;
15. same-name collision in the same state.

Expand to 100 stratified cases before pilot execution and 200 before national-scale execution.

* REVIEW Evaluation Gates

A release candidate must satisfy:

- verified-name precision at least 0.99;
- preferred verified-name precision at least 0.995;
- zero known role-confusion publications in the benchmark;
- every verified result has resolvable claim-to-evidence links;
- every measured alcohol value is distinguished from imputed values;
- copied-source clusters do not satisfy independence requirements;
- all negative controls abstain or reject correctly;
- cancellation terminates bounded workers;
- replay with fixed input and configuration reproduces the decision or records external-source drift;
- cost and request use remain inside declared limits.

Coverage, recall, and throughput are reported but cannot override the precision gate.

* REVIEW Failure and Recovery

- FARS download failure: retain release state and retry with bounded backoff.
- Invalid package hash: reject the release.
- Search unavailable: record blocked source and try a declared fallback.
- Page unavailable: invoke archive recovery once.
- Extraction disagreement: retain both values and block automatic publication if material.
- No official corroboration: remain probable or unresolved.
- Reviewer disagreement: abstain and request follow-up.
- Budget exhaustion: return a bounded partial packet.
- Publication write conflict: stop and require idempotent replay.
- Later correction: reopen review and produce a superseding or retracted claim.
- Model or policy change: rerun the benchmark before promotion.

* Footnotes and Glossary

[fn:actor-pipeline] Actor pipeline: A sequence and graph of small independent software workers that exchange structured messages while supervisors enforce ordering, limits, retries, and failure handling.

[fn:fars] Fatality Analysis Reporting System, or FARS: A national census of qualifying fatal motor-vehicle crashes maintained by the United States National Highway Traffic Safety Administration.

[fn:probabilistic-linkage] Probabilistic linkage: A statistical method for estimating whether records from separate sources describe the same event or person by measuring the information in their agreements and disagreements.

[fn:calibration] Calibration: Testing whether decisions reported at a probability level, such as 99 percent, are actually correct at approximately that rate on labeled examples.

[fn:blocking] Blocking: Restricting candidate comparisons to broad matches such as the same state and date window so the system does not compare every possible record pair.

[fn:abstention] Abstention: Returning unresolved rather than forcing a decision when evidence is weak or contradictory.

[fn:content-hash] Content hash: A fixed-length digest computed from file or page content so later processing can detect whether the exact input changed.

[fn:idempotent] Idempotent: Safe to repeat without creating duplicate or conflicting results.

[fn:legal-status] Legal status: The precise procedural state supported by public records, such as arrested, charged, convicted, acquitted, dismissed, or sentenced.

[fn:source-lineage] Source lineage: A record of whether several publications came from independent reporting, a shared official release, syndication, copying, or an updated version of the same source.
