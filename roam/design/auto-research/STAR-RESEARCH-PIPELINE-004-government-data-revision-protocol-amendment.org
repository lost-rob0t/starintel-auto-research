* REVIEW Objective

Amend =STAR-RESEARCH-PIPELINE-003= so its ownership, messages, routing, scheduling, leases, replay, review, and completion semantics can execute revisioned jurisdiction coverage without mutable logical-ID shortcuts or multi-writer state.

This amendment preserves the parent supervision architecture. After approval it supersedes conflicting protocol-v1 fields and state rules.

* REVIEW Problems Corrected

The parent v1 design:

- carries logical source-profile IDs through commands, events, results, checkpoints, routing, and scheduling
- omits deployment, search-plan, subfamily-decision, gap, route-assessment, review-decision, and coverage-gate revisions
- has no serialized route-assessment or atomic completion owner
- has no independent immutable review record
- risks two authorities writing the same profile/assessment review state
- merges durable logical jobs with leases issued only after dispatch
- lacks per-attempt fencing tokens
- cannot pin applicability contract, policy, manifest, and runtime snapshots into historical decisions

* REVIEW Normative Status

This node defines protocol v1.1. Both =PIPELINE-003= and this amendment require approval before implementation. Protocol v1 remains readable for migration and historical replay only after cutover.

* REVIEW Authority Changes

Add pinned state owners:

- Source Deployment Authority
- Search Plan Authority
- Gap Authority
- Route Assessment Authority
- Coverage Gate Authority
- Independent Review Authority

Existing identity/profile authorities continue owning their subject revisions. The Independent Review Authority never mutates those subjects; it owns separate review-decision revisions. The Coverage Ledger stores observations/materialized views and cannot synthesize canonical route or jurisdiction completion.

Root readiness requires replay completion for identity, deployment, profile, search-plan, gap, route-assessment, coverage-gate, review, adapter registry, raw artifact, rate/lease, and coverage authorities.

* REVIEW Durable Document Families

Required families:

- government unit/component records
- deployment and profile revisions
- applicability-contract revisions
- search-plan, gap, subfamily-decision, and route-assessment revisions
- independent review-decision revisions
- jurisdiction/wave/state coverage-gate revisions
- policy and adapter-manifest/runtime snapshots
- adapter-job revisions and adapter-attempt records
- retrieval events, raw artifacts, evidence, and checkpoints

Every subject transition is a new subject revision under one subject owner. Every human review transition is a new separate review-decision revision under the Independent Review Authority.

* REVIEW Independent Review Decision

#+begin_src lisp
(defstruct government-data-review-decision-revision
  review-id review-revision-id prior-review-revision-id
  supersedes-review-revision-ids
  subject-kind subject-revision-id decision
  reviewer-id reviewer-generation
  independence-policy-id conflict-check-result
  evidence-ids notes decided-at created-at)
#+end_src

The review authority:

- validates subject immutability and existence
- rejects self-review and policy conflicts
- records reviewer identity/generation and conflict result
- never writes subject fields
- creates a new review revision for review change or invalidation

Current reviewed state is a materialized join between a subject revision and its latest applicable review decision.

* REVIEW Atomic Coverage Gate

#+begin_src lisp
(defstruct government-data-coverage-gate-revision
  gate-id gate-revision-id prior-gate-revision-id
  supersedes-gate-revision-ids
  gate-kind scope-id run-id wave-id
  applicability-contract-id applicability-contract-version
  applicability-contract-review-revision-id
  policy-snapshot-id adapter-manifest-set-hash runtime-environment-set-hash
  route-assessment-revision-ids route-assessment-review-revision-ids
  required-subject-review-revision-ids
  blocker-ids status basis-hash decided-at decider-id created-at)
#+end_src

Gate kinds are jurisdiction completion, wave exit, and state completion. Only a reviewed =:passed= jurisdiction-completion gate may drive catalog =DONE=.

The Coverage Gate Authority:

- binds the exact ordered sixteen-route assessment/review set
- validates contract, policy, manifest/runtime, freshness, and required subject reviews
- calculates and verifies =basis-hash=
- creates a new gate on expiry, evidence change, review invalidation, or contract/policy migration
- never infers completion from mutable “latest” queries

The Independent Review Authority separately reviews the gate revision. Gate decider and gate reviewer must satisfy independence policy.

* REVIEW Job and Attempt Separation

A durable adapter job revision binds logical work, exact profile/proposal/search-plan revisions, contract, policy, adapter/manifest/runtime versions, bounds, artifact policy, and idempotency key. It contains no future lease.

#+begin_src lisp
(defstruct government-data-adapter-attempt
  attempt-id attempt-number job-revision-id prior-attempt-id
  rate-lease-id rate-lease-generation rate-lease-fence
  rate-lease-key-hash worker-generation started-at deadline input-hash)
#+end_src

After job validation the dispatcher acquires a lease and creates an attempt. Retry creates a new attempt/fence. Logical scope changes create a new job revision.

* REVIEW Checkpoint v1.1

#+begin_src lisp
(defstruct government-data-checkpoint-v1.1
  protocol command-id job-revision-id attempt-id
  source-profile-revision-id deployment-revision-id
  adapter-id adapter-version adapter-manifest-hash runtime-environment-hash
  partition cursor page newest-source-time oldest-source-time
  object-count byte-count request-count artifact-ids evidence-ids
  completed-partitions outstanding-partitions
  prior-checkpoint-id checkpoint-hash policy-snapshot-id configuration-hash)
#+end_src

Checkpoints never advance beyond committed artifacts/evidence. Resume creates a new attempt referencing an immutable checkpoint.

* REVIEW Routing

| Work | Serialization or read key |
|------|---------------------------|
| deployment/profile/search-plan/gap writes | logical subject ID for one-writer serialization |
| subfamily decisions | unit + route + subfamily + contract version |
| route assessments | unit + route + contract version |
| coverage gates | gate kind + scope ID + contract/policy |
| review decisions | subject kind + subject revision ID |
| acquisition cursor ordering | profile revision + partition |
| schedules | reviewed profile revision + policy snapshot |
| coverage materialization | scope + contract/policy + reviewed gate revision |

Reads and emitted decisions pin exact revisions even when write routing uses logical IDs.

* REVIEW State Ownership Boundaries

- Deployment/Profile/Search Plan/Gap/Route Assessment authorities own only their subject revisions.
- Independent Review owns only review decisions.
- Coverage Gate owns only aggregate gate revisions.
- Coverage Ledger owns observations and derived views only.
- No authority may directly set another authority’s canonical state.

* REVIEW Scheduler and Lease Changes

Scheduled work pins reviewed profile revision, deployment revision, policy, manifest/runtime hashes, checkpoint, due reason, and freshness basis. If any pinned current review changes before dispatch, the scheduler replans rather than following latest silently.

The Rate and Lease Authority issues ID, key hash, generation, fence, issue time, expiry, and close state. Every request event carries the attempt fence. Stale fences are rejected.

* REVIEW Artifact and Checkpoint Ordering

1. validate job
2. acquire lease
3. create attempt
4. request
5. reserve/stream artifact
6. verify bytes/hash
7. commit artifact/retrieval event
8. commit evidence/proposals
9. commit checkpoint
10. close lease
11. terminal result

Success is invalid with unexplained truncation, uncommitted artifacts, unreconciled counts, or expired attempt fence.

* REVIEW Protocol Migration

- v1 logical-ID commands remain readable but cannot create current canonical coverage decisions
- explicit migration selects and records exact revisions
- existing profile versions become immutable profile revisions with aliases
- coverage observations do not become assessments or gates automatically
- old gaps become first gap revisions and require independent review
- no v1 review field becomes an approved review decision automatically
- no jurisdiction becomes complete without a new reviewed coverage gate
- mixed v1/v1.1 is read-only or migration-only after cutover

* REVIEW Capability Changes

Add propose/commit capabilities for deployment, search plan, gap, subfamily decision, route assessment, coverage gate, and independent review, plus lease-fenced adapter attempts. Only owning authorities receive commit capabilities.

* REVIEW Deterministic Fixtures

- v1 logical-ID current-completion rejection
- self-review/conflict rejection
- reviewed subject without subject mutation
- invalidated review preserving subject history
- full sixteen-route atomic gate and basis hash
- route change after old gate without replay mutation
- gate expiry from stale negative evidence
- coverage observations without reviewed gate cannot create completion
- profile change invalidating scheduled work
- retry with same job/new attempt fence
- stale-fence rejection
- crash after artifact commit/before checkpoint
- explicit v1 profile/gap/review/gate migration

* REVIEW Acceptance Criteria

1. Every canonical decision pins exact revisions.
2. Subject owners and review owner are disjoint.
3. Route Assessment, Coverage Gate, and Independent Review have explicit owners.
4. Coverage Ledger cannot synthesize completion.
5. Catalog =DONE= requires a reviewed passed atomic gate.
6. Jobs and lease-bound attempts are separate and fenced.
7. Checkpoints cannot outrun artifact/evidence commits.
8. Scheduler jobs never silently follow latest state.
9. v1 migration cannot create unreviewed completion.
10. Ohio contracts execute without protocol exceptions after approval.
