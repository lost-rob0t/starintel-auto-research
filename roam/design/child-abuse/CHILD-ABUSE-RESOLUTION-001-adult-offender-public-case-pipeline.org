* REVIEW Objective

Define actors and message contracts for severe child-abuse public-case research without running AutoDig, selecting real cases, crawling sources, ingesting a dataset, or publishing names.

The architecture may later support evidence-backed records about publicly documented adult defendants. It must never identify child victims, child survivors, minor witnesses, or minor alleged offenders.

* REVIEW User Directive

The user explicitly directed:

- actors only;
- no AutoDig for this domain;
- do not expose child names;
- child-name exposure would abuse the child further;
- document the request as a durable project rule.

This directive is implemented by [[file:CHILD-ABUSE-POLICY-001-child-identity-firewall.org][CHILD-ABUSE-POLICY-001 Child Identity Firewall]]. The firewall is approved and supersedes weaker generic evidence-preservation behavior.

* REVIEW Invariants

1. No live source retrieval occurs under this design review.
2. No AutoDig run, dataset, case packet, target queue, or monitoring job is created.
3. No real child identity enters an actor request, response, log, prompt, fixture, filename, or document.
4. Child identities remain prohibited even when a source has already made them public.
5. Only adults with a supported public legal role may become future named targets.
6. A person whose adult status is unresolved is treated as a child and cannot be named.
7. The adult defendant and child victim are separate roles; proximity in source text cannot establish either role.
8. Child records are case-local pseudonyms and cannot be linked across cases.
9. Raw source content is unavailable to downstream actors until child identifiers are removed.
10. Detailed sexual or medical narratives are removed unless indispensable to a legal element and approved for restricted use.
11. Every stage may abstain or stop.
12. Privacy failure overrides evidence confidence and blocks publication.
13. Legal status is represented exactly and temporally.
14. Synthetic fixtures are the only permitted inputs before separate implementation approval.
15. No actor may expand to relatives, schools, households, social accounts, or associates.

* REVIEW Non-Execution Boundary

This design describes actors but does not authorize actors to execute against real sources.

All network capabilities default to disabled:

#+begin_src json
{
  "network": false,
  "web_search": false,
  "browser": false,
  "archive_search": false,
  "court_fetch": false,
  "government_portal_fetch": false,
  "public_records_send": false,
  "autodig": false,
  "canonical_write": false
}
#+end_src

The only enabled capabilities during design validation are:

- read synthetic fixtures;
- emit protocol events;
- apply deterministic redaction;
- validate synthetic adult status and legal status;
- generate nonpublic test reports;
- scan test exports for prohibited values.

A later implementation proposal must name each capability it seeks to enable. Approval of this design does not enable live retrieval.

* REVIEW Actor Contracts

** Operator Authorization Gate

Purpose: reject execution modes not explicitly approved.

Consumes:

- requested operation;
- capability set;
- project policy version.

Produces:

- authorized design-test request;
- denied-operation event.

Rules:

- =autodig= is always denied;
- live network capabilities are denied;
- only synthetic fixture identifiers are accepted;
- denied requests do not echo prohibited child identifiers.

** Case Scope Gate

Purpose: restrict the design to severe child-abuse public-case patterns.

Consumes:

- synthetic case classification;
- synthetic outcome and source classes.

Produces:

- eligible test branch;
- ineligible terminal result;
- unresolved classification.

Eligible synthetic classes:

- fatal abuse or neglect;
- near fatality;
- serious bodily injury;
- permanent impairment;
- severe starvation, poisoning, burning, torture, or medical neglect;
- publicly charged sexual abuse represented without graphic victim details.

Excluded classes:

- custody disputes;
- unsubstantiated referrals;
- minor injuries;
- school discipline;
- social-media accusations;
- family-court-only allegations;
- ordinary parenting disputes;
- cases lacking public adult legal action.

** Child Identity Input Firewall

Purpose: prevent prohibited child identity from entering the run.

Consumes:

- operator input;
- fixture payload;
- attachment metadata.

Produces:

- sanitized request;
- privacy rejection event.

Rules:

- remove child names, initials, exact birth dates, addresses, schools, photos, handles, and contacts;
- reject rather than guess when safe rewriting is impossible;
- never place the rejected token in logs;
- adult names are allowed only in synthetic fixtures and only when marked adult.

** Synthetic Fixture Adapter

Purpose: provide deterministic test cases without real people.

Consumes:

- fixture identifier;
- fixture version.

Produces:

- synthetic official release;
- synthetic court filing;
- synthetic local-news story;
- expected redaction and legal-status outputs.

Rules:

- all names are obviously fictional;
- child names remain prohibited even in fixtures; use placeholders such as =[CHILD]=;
- no fixture copies a real case narrative;
- fixture labels remain inaccessible to scoring actors until evaluation.

** Source Request Policy Actor

Purpose: decide whether a proposed future retrieval request would be permitted.

Consumes:

- source class;
- proposed query or URL template;
- capability policy.

Produces:

- permit, rewrite, or deny decision;
- required redaction profile.

Rules:

- deny child-name searches;
- deny reverse-family and associate expansion;
- deny obituary, fundraiser, school, or social-account searching;
- deny search requests when the adult legal role is absent;
- in current design mode, route every permitted request to the fake adapter.

** Child-Safe Query Rewriter

Purpose: remove child identifiers from query plans.

Consumes:

- synthetic event facts;
- adult candidate facts after event-based discovery;
- source restrictions.

Produces:

- nonidentifying event query;
- adult official-record confirmation query;
- denied-query event.

Allowed query features:

- month or date;
- county or state;
- public agency;
- severe outcome class;
- adult candidate name only after event-based discovery;
- public charge and case terms.

Prohibited query features:

- child name or initials;
- school or daycare;
- home address;
- sibling or parent discovery from child identity;
- obituary or fundraiser terms;
- child social account.

** Fake Retrieval Adapter

Purpose: simulate source retrieval while proving actor contracts.

Consumes:

- permitted synthetic query;
- fixture source identifier.

Produces:

- synthetic content;
- source metadata;
- retrieval event.

Rules:

- no network access;
- one immutable output per fixture version;
- simulate redirects, PDFs, URL slugs, image captions, and copied stories;
- include adversarial child identifiers so downstream redaction can be tested.

** Raw Content Quarantine

Purpose: prevent unredacted content from reaching normal actors.

Consumes:

- fake retrieval result.

Produces:

- quarantine artifact reference;
- redaction task.

Rules:

- only the redactor can read quarantined content;
- raw content is never indexed, embedded, logged, graphed, or exported;
- public repositories receive no quarantine artifacts;
- fixture teardown deletes transient raw content.

** Child Identifier Redactor

Purpose: remove direct child identity.

Consumes:

- quarantined content;
- metadata and attachment names.

Produces:

- redacted text;
- redacted metadata;
- category counts;
- privacy warnings.

Redaction categories:

- child name and initials;
- exact birth date and exact age;
- address and precise location;
- school, daycare, classroom, team, or club;
- child photo, caption, alt text, and filename;
- social handle and contact details;
- sibling and minor witness identity;
- quoted child statement;
- detailed medical or sexual narrative.

** Indirect Identification Detector

Purpose: detect combinations that could identify a child without naming them.

Consumes:

- redacted content;
- proposed normalized facts;
- geography and age precision.

Produces:

- safe, generalize, or reject decision;
- indirect-identification reasons.

Rules:

- generalize exact ages into approved bands;
- generalize location to county or state;
- remove distinctive school, family, medical, and event details;
- reject when several remaining facts make the child readily identifiable;
- never reconstruct a removed name.

** Severe Harm Classifier

Purpose: classify the synthetic case outcome without determining guilt.

Consumes:

- redacted official facts;
- synthetic medical and legal outcome fields.

Produces:

- fatality, near-fatality, serious-injury, charged-sexual-abuse, or out-of-scope class;
- source-supported reasons.

Rules:

- severity does not identify an offender;
- ambiguous severity yields unresolved;
- graphic detail is not retained merely to justify severity.

** Adult Status Verifier

Purpose: establish that a potential named target was at least eighteen at the event.

Consumes:

- redacted source packet;
- adult candidate record.

Produces:

- adult, minor, or unresolved decision;
- age evidence locator.

Rules:

- minor and unresolved decisions block naming;
- never infer adult status from appearance, occupation, parenthood, or marital status;
- use explicit age, date-of-birth calculation from an authoritative adult record, or a court statement that the person was tried as an adult;
- do not retain unnecessary exact birth dates.

** Adult Role Extractor

Purpose: identify the adult's exact public role.

Consumes:

- redacted source packet.

Produces:

- defendant, charged adult, convicted offender, nonoffending caregiver, witness, reporter, official, or ambiguous role;
- supporting evidence.

Rules:

- only a supported defendant or offender role proceeds;
- nonoffending caregiver and ambiguous roles remain unnamed;
- article proximity is not role evidence;
- several adults in one case remain separate candidates.

** Legal Status Extractor

Purpose: preserve procedural status and lifecycle.

Consumes:

- redacted court, prosecutor, police, and reporting artifacts.

Produces:

- arrest, charge, indictment, plea, verdict, sentence, acquittal, dismissal, appeal, or correction event;
- exact date and authority when safe;
- contradiction records.

Rules:

- allegations remain allegations;
- arrest without public charge does not authorize publication;
- later dismissals, acquittals, and corrections supersede earlier status;
- child identity never appears in event text.

** Case Evidence Normalizer

Purpose: convert redacted evidence into source, evidence, claim, legal-case, event, and adult-person candidates.

Consumes:

- redacted artifacts;
- role and legal-status results;
- severe-harm classification.

Produces:

- noncanonical candidate documents;
- provenance links;
- contradiction set.

Rules:

- child person uses only a case-local pseudonym and age band;
- source excerpts use =[CHILD]=;
- no raw artifact path enters candidate documents;
- synthetic mode cannot write canonical documents.

** Adult Candidate Linker

Purpose: decide whether several public adult references describe the same adult in the same case.

Consumes:

- adult candidate records;
- case identifiers;
- legal-status evidence.

Produces:

- same-adult, different-adult, or unresolved decision;
- feature report.

Rules:

- no child features are linkage keys;
- no household graph is created;
- cross-case adult linkage is deferred;
- name alone is insufficient when several adults share a name.

** Independent Evidence Reviewer

Purpose: review adult role, case match, and legal status without inheriting the linker conclusion.

Consumes:

- candidate packet;
- redacted sources;
- contradictions.

Produces:

- approve, reject, or abstain;
- unsupported claims;
- follow-up requirements.

Rules:

- reviews exact legal status;
- rejects copied-source pseudo-corroboration;
- checks nonoffending-adult confusion;
- cannot waive child privacy rules.

** Independent Privacy Reviewer

Purpose: independently evaluate direct and indirect child identification.

Consumes:

- complete proposed packet;
- redaction report;
- export preview.

Produces:

- approve, reject, or require-generalization;
- leakage findings.

Rules:

- default is reject when uncertain;
- checks text, metadata, identifiers, URLs, attachment names, graph labels, and generated prose;
- may require removal of analytically interesting facts;
- cannot be overridden by the evidence reviewer.

** Publication Policy Gate

Purpose: make the only decision about whether a future adult record could be published.

Consumes:

- adult verification;
- role and legal-status result;
- evidence review;
- privacy review;
- contradiction state;
- export scan preview.

Produces:

- structurally-publishable, nonpublic-candidate, rejected, or unresolved decision.

Rules:

- current design mode always returns =nonpublic-design-output=;
- live publication remains disabled;
- adult status, formal public legal role, evidence approval, privacy approval, and zero leakage are all mandatory;
- one failed prerequisite blocks the packet.

** Child-Safe StarIntel Writer

Purpose: validate the shape of future documents without writing them canonically.

Consumes:

- structurally-publishable synthetic packet.

Produces:

- temporary test documents;
- schema diagnostics.

Future document pattern:

#+begin_src text
adult public defendant
  --defendant_in-->
public legal case
  --concerns-->
child-victim-01
#+end_src

Rules:

- child label is case-local;
- no =resolved_as= relation targets a child;
- no child identity or stable cross-case identifier exists;
- current mode writes only to temporary test storage.

** Export Leakage Scanner

Purpose: scan every serialized output surface.

Consumes:

- JSON, JSONL, Org, HTML, EPUB, graph, search-index, log, and error-output previews.

Produces:

- pass or fail;
- prohibited-token and risky-combination findings.

Rules:

- scan content and metadata;
- scan URL slugs and attachment titles;
- scan generated summaries;
- a missing scan is a failure;
- any known child-identity leak fails the release.

** Audit and Retraction Actor

Purpose: prove that corrections remove prohibited output and preserve adult legal lifecycle.

Consumes:

- test publication packet;
- simulated correction, dismissal, or privacy incident.

Produces:

- supersession or retraction test record;
- cleanup verification;
- incident report.

Rules:

- child identity must not appear in incident reports;
- caches and indexes are included in cleanup tests;
- adult dismissals and acquittals are prominently preserved;
- retraction cannot silently delete provenance history.

* REVIEW Synthetic Fixture Suite

Required fixtures:

1. official release contains a fictional child name;
2. initials appear throughout a court filing;
3. child name appears in a URL slug;
4. child name appears in PDF metadata;
5. photograph filename and alt text identify a child;
6. school, address, and exact birth date permit indirect identification;
7. obituary reveals siblings and caregivers;
8. fundraiser contains medical narrative;
9. adult defendant and child share a surname;
10. nonoffending caregiver is named more prominently than defendant;
11. two adult defendants exist in one case;
12. adult age is unresolved;
13. alleged offender is a minor;
14. arrest occurred but no public charge followed;
15. charge was dismissed;
16. adult was acquitted;
17. copied stories create false source independence;
18. model attempts to reconstruct the child's name;
19. graph exporter creates a stable child identifier;
20. error log echoes rejected input;
21. child identity appears only inside an attachment title;
22. exact age plus tiny locality permits re-identification;
23. sexual-abuse source contains unnecessary graphic details;
24. synthetic case is out of severe-harm scope;
25. request attempts to enable AutoDig.

Expected outcome for fixture 25: immediate denial before any other actor executes.

* REVIEW Evaluation Gates

A design-test release must demonstrate:

- zero child names in all outputs;
- zero child-name queries;
- zero exact child birth dates;
- zero child photos or image identifiers;
- zero stable cross-case child identifiers;
- zero named minor alleged offenders;
- zero AutoDig execution paths;
- zero network calls;
- zero canonical writes;
- all legal-status corrections represented;
- all adult-age-unknown cases blocked;
- every export surface scanned;
- every fixture deterministic and replayable.

There is no acceptable child-identity leakage threshold above zero.

* REVIEW Implementation Sequence

This sequence is a future proposal, not authorization to implement live research:

1. Define schemas for requests, events, redaction reports, review results, and test documents.
2. Build synthetic fixtures with no real names.
3. Implement the Operator Authorization Gate.
4. Implement the Child Identity Input Firewall.
5. Implement deterministic direct-identifier redaction.
6. Implement indirect-identification rules.
7. Implement fake retrieval and quarantine.
8. Implement adult-status, adult-role, and legal-status validators.
9. Implement evidence and privacy reviewers.
10. Implement temporary child-safe document generation.
11. Implement export scanning across every supported format.
12. Run the complete privacy suite.
13. Review results with the user.
14. Do not add live search or AutoDig unless the user makes a separate explicit request.

* REVIEW Deferred and Prohibited Work

Deferred pending separate approval:

- live official-source retrieval;
- court adapters;
- prosecutor and police adapters;
- aggregate NCANDS, NIBRS, or NVSS analysis;
- state disclosure registries;
- real-case benchmarking.

Prohibited under the current user directive:

- AutoDig case research;
- child-name search;
- child-name storage;
- child identity resolution;
- cross-case child linkage;
- family or associate expansion;
- school and social-account discovery;
- child image collection;
- obituary and fundraiser harvesting;
- public raw source storage;
- named minor-offender records.

* REVIEW Acceptance Criteria

This design is ready for approval when:

- the user directive is quoted accurately in the research node;
- the Child Identity Firewall remains approved;
- actors have no enabled live-network or AutoDig capability;
- every child-data path passes through redaction and privacy review;
- the fixture suite covers direct and indirect identification;
- adult naming requires adult status and formal public legal status;
- no implementation slot is occupied;
- CI validates Org-roam links and publication artifacts.

* Footnotes and Glossary

[fn:actor] Actor: A small independent software worker that owns a limited responsibility and communicates through structured messages.

[fn:privacy-firewall] Privacy firewall: Mandatory checks at several system boundaries that reject, remove, or generalize prohibited identifying information.

[fn:quarantine] Quarantine: A restricted temporary area where untrusted raw content is isolated until it has been sanitized.

[fn:indirect-identification] Indirect identification: Recognizing a person from a combination of details such as age, school, location, family structure, and medical facts even when their name is absent.
