* REVIEW Objective

Define one national-compatible immutable contract for Ohio state, county, component, shared, regional, delegated, API, dashboard, document, archive, applicability, gap, review, and completion records without county schema forks.

* REVIEW Invariants

1. Workers emit proposals and evidence, never verified canonical state.
2. Subject revisions and review revisions have disjoint one-writer authorities.
3. Every subject-state transition creates a new immutable subject revision.
4. Human review creates a separate immutable review-decision revision and never mutates the subject.
5. One stable review stream per subject revision is CAS-linear; timestamps never resolve forks.
6. Every canonical revision declares logical ID, revision ID, predecessor, and superseded revisions.
7. Route assessments bind exact profile, deployment, search-plan, subfamily-decision, gap, evidence, contract, and policy revisions.
8. Jurisdiction/wave/state completion uses one atomic gate revision binding exact subject and review revisions.
9. Gate validity is time-bounded and deterministically hashed.
10. Shared deployments do not prove jurisdiction delegation.
11. A profile does not imply route completion.
12. Negative findings are evidence-bearing and expiring.
13. =:not-applicable= requires positive authority or service-topology evidence.
14. Later revisions never alter older replay.
15. Browser/OCR evidence requires replayable artifacts.
16. Personal-data/access restrictions precede acquisition authorization.

* REVIEW Independent Review Stream

#+begin_src lisp
(defstruct review-decision-revision
  review-id review-revision-id prior-review-revision-id
  supersedes-review-revision-ids
  subject-kind subject-revision-id decision
  expected-prior-review-revision-id
  reviewer-id reviewer-generation
  independence-policy-id conflict-check-result
  evidence-ids notes decided-at created-at)
#+end_src

Subject kinds include deployment, profile, applicability contract, policy snapshot, manifest set, runtime set, search plan, gap, subfamily decision, route assessment, and coverage gate.

Rules:

- one stable =review-id= exists per subject revision
- absence of a review head means unreviewed
- commits use compare-and-swap against =expected-prior-review-revision-id=
- only one unsuperseded head is valid
- concurrent forks are rejected, never timestamp-merged
- decisions are =:reviewed=, =:changes-requested=, =:rejected=, or =:invalidated=
- subject owners cannot self-review
- changes requested/rejection normally produces a new subject revision
- invalidation creates a new review revision and preserves the subject

Current review state is the decision at the unique unsuperseded review head. “Latest applicable” timestamp queries are forbidden.

Every subject used by completion requires a separate reviewed review decision.

Route states are unassessed, candidate-found, verified, verified-partial, not-published, records-request-required, authentication-required, paywalled, blocked, retired, ambiguous, and not-applicable. Candidate, ambiguous, and unassessed are nonterminal.

Verified requires reviewed required subfamilies. Verified-partial requires explicit scope and current reviewed gaps.

Negative maximum ages are 30 days high, 90 days medium, 180 days low, and next effective date or 180 days for legal-versioned sources.

* REVIEW Atomic Coverage Gate and Canonical Hash

#+begin_src lisp
(defstruct coverage-gate-revision
  gate-id gate-revision-id prior-gate-revision-id
  supersedes-gate-revision-ids
  gate-kind scope-id run-id wave-id
  applicability-contract-id applicability-contract-version
  applicability-contract-review-revision-id
  policy-snapshot-id policy-review-revision-id
  adapter-manifest-set-hash manifest-set-review-revision-id
  runtime-environment-set-hash runtime-set-review-revision-id
  route-assessment-revision-ids route-assessment-review-revision-ids
  required-subject-review-revision-ids blocker-ids status
  basis-schema-version hash-algorithm basis-as-of basis-hash
  freshness-deadline valid-through
  decided-at decider-id created-at)
#+end_src

Gate kinds are jurisdiction completion, wave exit, and state completion. Status is passed, blocked, expired, or superseded.

Canonical basis rules follow =PIPELINE-004=:

1. Sort route pairs by applicability-contract route order, then subject kind, logical ID, and revision ID.
2. Normalize strings as UTF-8, timestamps as UTC RFC 3339, explicit nulls, and key-sorted maps.
3. Use the declared basis schema and hash algorithm; default SHA-256.
4. Evaluate freshness at =basis-as-of=.
5. Set =freshness-deadline= to the earliest reassessment/expiry among bound subjects.
6. Set =valid-through= to the earliest contract, policy, subject, review, or freshness validity limit.

Current completion immediately becomes false when current time exceeds =valid-through= or a bound review head is invalidated. It does not wait for an explicit expiry write. The expired gate revision is still recorded for history.

A reviewed passed jurisdiction gate is the only subject that may satisfy catalog =DONE=.

* REVIEW Validation

Reject:

- mutable or unresolved subject references
- review forks or wrong expected review head
- self-review or failed independence checks
- not-applicable without positive evidence
- negative decisions without completed reviewed search plans/gaps
- stale evidence at gate basis time
- nonterminal route states in a passed gate
- missing route/review pair among the sixteen routes
- basis schema/hash mismatch
- invalid/expired contract, policy, subject, review, manifest, or runtime set
- use of a passed gate after =valid-through=
- Coverage Ledger completion without a reviewed passed gate

* REVIEW Parent Protocol Requirement

=PIPELINE-004= must be approved and reconciled with =PIPELINE-003= before implementation.

* REVIEW Acceptance Criteria

1. All canonical subjects and reviews are immutable revisions.
2. Review streams are CAS-linear and have one current head.
3. Subject and review writers are disjoint.
4. All sixteen routes are machine-readable.
5. Route assessments bind exact reviewed subject revisions.
6. Jurisdiction/wave/state completion uses canonical atomic gates.
7. Gate hashes and validity are deterministic and time-bounded.
8. Catalog =DONE= requires a currently valid reviewed passed gate.
9. Negative findings expire.
10. Browser/OCR evidence is replayable.
11. Protocol and payload versions are unambiguous.
12. Parent protocol alignment precedes implementation.

* REVIEW Required Fixtures

- review CAS conflict and self-review rejection
- invalidated review preserving subject history
- canonical hash with unordered-equivalent inputs
- hash rejection for route/null/timestamp differences
- full sixteen-route Franklin gate
- automatic current-status expiry at valid-through
- route change after old gate without old replay mutation
- gate supersession after policy/contract migration
- coverage observations without reviewed gate cannot create DONE
- subject/review/gate migration from protocol v1
