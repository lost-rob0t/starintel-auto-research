* Purpose

This index tracks the StarIntel automatic-research control plane[fn:control-plane]. It separates durable knowledge and design approval from transient candidate execution.

* Approved Domain Policies

- [[file:../../design/child-abuse/CHILD-ABUSE-POLICY-001-child-identity-firewall.org][CHILD-ABUSE-POLICY-001 Child Identity Firewall]]

Status: approved by nsaspy on 2026-07-28. It prohibits child names, child-name searches, stable cross-case child identifiers, identifying victim metadata, raw public child-identifying source artifacts, and named minor alleged offenders. It also records that the child-abuse project is actor-only and must not run AutoDig.

* Design Under Review

- [[file:../../design/auto-research/STAR-RESEARCH-PIPELINE-002-deep-research-execution-adapters.org][STAR-RESEARCH-PIPELINE-002 Deep Research Execution Adapters]]
- [[file:../../design/drunk-drivers/DRUNK-DRIVER-RESOLUTION-001-fars-to-name-actor-pipeline.org][DRUNK-DRIVER-RESOLUTION-001 FARS-to-Name Actor Pipeline]]
- [[file:../../design/child-abuse/CHILD-ABUSE-RESOLUTION-001-adult-offender-public-case-pipeline.org][CHILD-ABUSE-RESOLUTION-001 Adult-Offender Public-Case Pipeline]]

Status: review. The deep-research adapter design keeps ADARD as the control plane and places Open Deep Research, GPT Researcher, STORM, PaperQA2, and browser JavaScript actors behind one bounded worker-adapter contract.

The FARS-to-name design applies that bounded-worker model to a specific public-record entity-resolution problem. It separates crash matching, driver-role matching, identity matching, independent review, and publication.

The child-abuse design is deliberately nonexecuting. It defines actor contracts and synthetic tests only. AutoDig, live search, real-case ingestion, and canonical publication are disabled. The only possible future named targets are publicly documented adults with supported legal roles; children remain unnamed and unlinkable across cases.

None of these designs enters the implementation slot until separately approved and the slot is free.

* Domain Research Projects

- [[file:../drunk-drivers/DRUNK-DRIVER-INDEX-000-national-crash-identity-resolution.org][DRUNK-DRIVER-INDEX-000 National Crash Identity Resolution]]
- [[file:../child-abuse/CHILD-ABUSE-INDEX-000-severe-case-adult-offender-research.org][CHILD-ABUSE-INDEX-000 Severe-Case Adult-Offender Research]]

The drunk-driver project is the first domain-specific consumer design for public-web identity resolution over an official anonymous national dataset. It must preserve ADARD budgets and evidence contracts while adding domain-specific legal-status, role-resolution, calibration, and publication rules.

The child-abuse project is an actor-design and privacy-validation project, not an automatic research run. Its approved Child Identity Firewall forbids using public child identities even when external sources reveal them. Its first allowable work is limited to schemas, synthetic fixtures, fake retrieval, deterministic redaction, adult legal-status validation, and export leakage scanning.

* Footnotes and Glossary

[fn:control-plane] Control plane: The part of a system that decides what work should run, applies policy, records state, and coordinates workers rather than performing the main data processing itself.

[fn:domain-gserver] Domain gserver: A supervised actor subtree responsible for one bounded area of work, with its own dispatchers, ingress, workers, health, and optional transport gateways.
