* Finding

*KEEP AS A RANKER, NOT TRUTH.* Graph anomaly-detection research explicitly studies unusual nodes, edges, subgraphs, and whole graphs. That supports structural rarity as a prioritization signal for Auto Research.

Rarity does not establish wrongdoing, importance, causation, or truth. A rare edge may simply reflect sparse data or a novel but harmless event. StarIntel should treat graph anomaly scores as triage features and combine them with provenance strength, source independence, temporal novelty, domain rules, and data-quality checks.

* Research Gaps

- Benchmark degree, motif, neighborhood, temporal novelty, and embedding-based rarity separately.
- Measure false-positive rates caused by incomplete ingest.
- Prefer interpretable baseline measures before graph neural networks.
