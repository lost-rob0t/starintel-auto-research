* Decision

On 2026-08-11, nsaspy explicitly approved each of the four public review pages listed above.

This approval accepts the architecture and research direction represented by those documents for implementation planning and dependency ordering.

Approval does not claim that implementation is complete, that acceptance tests have passed, or that runtime, performance, security, deployment, and migration gates have already been satisfied. Those remain evidence-bearing implementation and validation requirements.

* Scope Boundaries

Approval of =QUASAR-INDEX-001= approves the index as a review, dependency, and migration map. It does not automatically approve every queued child document linked from that index. In particular, child =PENDING= and =BLOCKED= states remain unchanged unless separately approved.

Approval of =QUASAR-RESEARCH-002= accepts the client-library stack direction as research guidance. Exact package versions, compatibility, performance limits, and replacement decisions remain subject to implementation evidence and lockfile validation.

Approval of =QUASAR-001= accepts the basic local-first typed graph editor architecture and its Quasar-owned action boundary. Protocol details and later scale or collaboration mechanisms remain governed by their dedicated design and conformance work.

Approval of =STAR-SERVER-000= accepts the composable domain-gserver architecture. This directly reaffirms the scoped domain-gserver direction already represented by the existing StarIntel architecture decision record; it does not imply that all required Sento extensions or transport behaviors are implemented.
