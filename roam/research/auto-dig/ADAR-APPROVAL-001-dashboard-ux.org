* Scope Condition

Approval is conditional on the reusable boundary captured in ADAR-001A:

- the UX is not owned by or hardcoded to the Auto-Dig repository;
- Quasar may expose the research workspace UX;
- Auto-Dig, ADARD/RAGE, and user-run compatible research repositories may provide backends/adapters;
- canonical documents and human approval remain authoritative in the backing workspace;
- repository-specific features are capability-discovered rather than assumed.

Where ADAR-001's old fixed repository implementation map conflicts with ADAR-001A, ADAR-001A wins.

* Implementation Authorization

The design is approved to move to implementation. RAGE/implementers may select the corresponding =roam/implement/auto-dig/= slot, but must consume ADAR-001A before making repository or backend assumptions.

Approval does not waive TDD, accessibility, performance, publication, security, or exact-head validation gates.
