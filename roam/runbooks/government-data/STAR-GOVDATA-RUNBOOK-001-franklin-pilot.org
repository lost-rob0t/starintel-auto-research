* Wave 3: Bounded Acquisition

For every eligible profile:

1. create one immutable job revision;
2. acquire a lease and create one attempt with a new fence;
3. issue bounded requests only after fence validation;
4. commit artifacts and durable delivery intents;
5. deliver and acknowledge retrieval-event and normalization work;
6. emit evidence and proposals;
7. commit checkpoints with acknowledged and pending delivery state;
8. reconcile counts, pages, cursors, dates, hashes, and truncation;
9. close the lease;
10. reject terminal success while any success-required delivery step is pending.

2. Commit route assessments for routes 1 through 15.
3. Bind exact unit, component, deployment, profile, search-plan, gap, evidence, artifact, retrieval-event, delivery-intent, policy, manifest, runtime, and applicability revisions.
4. Independently review every terminal subject.
5. Derive route 16 from the complete cross-cutting bindings.
6. Record gaps and reassessment deadlines for every partial, negative, blocked, OCR, manual-review, or records-request state.

* Wave 5: Atomic Completion Gate

The Coverage Gate Authority computes one deterministic Franklin jurisdiction gate.

The gate must pin:

- exact unit and component revisions and reviews
- source and applicability contract revisions and reviews
- policy, manifest, and runtime revisions and reviews
- all sixteen route assessments and reviews
- every required profile, deployment, search, decision, gap, artifact, retrieval event, delivery intent, evidence, and checkpoint revision
- basis schema, canonical ordering, hash algorithm, basis time, basis hash, freshness deadline, and =valid-through=

Reject the gate when:

- a route is candidate, ambiguous, unassessed, stale, or missing review;
- a delivery intent is absent, orphaned, quarantined without resolution, or pending a success-required step;
- a review head changed or was invalidated;
- identity or applicability revisions were superseded;
- canonical hash validation fails;
- =valid-through= is absent or expired.

* Replay Gate

1. Start from empty mailboxes and replay durable state.
2. Replay pending delivery intents before readiness.
3. Recompute authority state hashes and the gate basis hash.
4. Require exact equality with the recorded accepted run.
5. Inject duplicate messages, reordered delivery, authority restart, stale references, stale fences, and both artifact crash windows.
6. Require the same canonical state and no duplicate authority revisions.

* Current Completion

A historical passed gate remains immutable. Current Franklin completion is true only while its exact gate and bound reviews, contracts, identities, delivery state, freshness, and validity remain current.

Current completion becomes false immediately after any bound invalidation or after =valid-through=, without waiting for another write.

* Live-Execution Stop Conditions

Stop and quarantine the affected scope when:

- access or privacy policy is unresolved;
- CAPTCHA, authentication, payment, or prohibited-action boundaries are encountered;
- artifact integrity fails;
- delivery replay cannot account for a committed artifact;
- source scope or legal meaning is ambiguous;
- a parser hides truncation or unreconciled counts;
- a required reviewer is not independent;
- a source, contract, identity, manifest, runtime, or policy revision changes mid-run.

A stopped scope creates explicit gaps and never silently becomes complete.
