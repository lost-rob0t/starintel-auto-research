* Government Data Acquisition Track

- [[file:../../todos/government-data/STAR-GOVDATA-TODO-000-jurisdiction-source-catalog.org][STAR-GOVDATA-TODO-000 State and County Source Catalog]]

=STAR-RESEARCH-003= defines the canonical registry, discovery, platform, acquisition, provenance, synchronization, failure, and coverage model.

Government units remain durable records and job keys rather than permanent actors. The catalog contains the 50 states, District of Columbia, Puerto Rico, and every 2025 Census county/county-equivalent GEOID.

Ohio is the first state design. Franklin County is the first complete county pilot.

The later violent-offender website is not part of the acquisition design. Franklin ingestion remains source-neutral and preserves source status, allegations, charges, dispositions, time, provenance, uncertainty, and review state without assigning offender labels.

* REVIEW Protocol Alignment Resolution

The Ohio review exposed protocol-v1 gaps in =PIPELINE-003=. Those gaps are formalized in =PIPELINE-004= rather than left as implicit Ohio exceptions.

The amendment requires:

- exact profile, deployment, search-plan, decision, gap, assessment, review, and coverage-gate revision IDs
- disjoint subject authorities and Independent Review Authority
- Route Assessment and Coverage Gate authorities
- atomic reviewed completion gates with basis hashes
- applicability-contract and policy-snapshot bindings
- revision-aware events, results, checkpoints, routing, and scheduling
- durable job and lease-bound attempt separation with fencing tokens

The amendment must be approved and reconciled with =PIPELINE-003= before Ohio or Franklin implementation.

* Franklin County Actor Track

The Franklin domain is authored in StarLang and compiled to Common Lisp.

Actor groups:

1. control and policy
2. jurisdiction and component identity
3. discovery and platform fingerprinting
4. acquisition dispatch, leases, generic adapters, Ohio adapters, and process workers
5. raw artifacts, retrieval events, hashes, metadata, and provenance
6. eight data-family normalization pools
7. applicability, route assessment, independent review, coverage, and gaps
8. scheduling, migration detection, reconciliation, quarantine, health, and metrics

The system is a supervised deployment, not one permanent actor per office, source, route, record, or person.

* StarLang Runtime Track

Common Lisp remains the sole StarLang implementation language.

The runtime design adds reusable ASDF systems for:

- actor protocol
- Sento compatibility
- bounded mailboxes
- supervision
- durable journals
- leases and fencing
- capabilities
- artifact custody
- adapter SDK
- HTTP and process ports
- canonical JSON
- XLSX extraction
- compiler and runtime assembly

The recommended repository boundary is a new dedicated =lost-rob0t/star-lang= repository after its name, license, maintainers, dependency locking, CI, and release policy are approved. =starintel-auto-research= remains the design/evidence repository; =starintel-server= later consumes released runtime systems.
