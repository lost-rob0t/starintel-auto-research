* Purpose

This index tracks the StarIntel national impaired-driving crash identity-resolution project.

The project keeps official anonymous crash data separate from named public-case records, then links them only through evidence-backed, independently reviewed resolution claims.

The named-case pipeline is not a general list of impaired drivers. It is bounded to another person's death caused or materially contributed to by the selected driver, or a narrow nonfatal exception requiring both serious injury causation and objectively documented severe impairment.

The hard formula is:

#+begin_src text
eligible = fatal_causation
        OR (serious_injury_causation AND severe_impairment)
#+end_src

The gate runs before web search and fails closed when harm, causation, or the exception evidence is missing.

* Current Decisions

- FARS is the national fatal-crash backbone.
- Anonymous FARS people are never renamed in place.
- Public-source identity resolution is probabilistic and may abstain.
- Event, driver role, person identity, harm, and causation are separate proofs.
- The Severe Harm Eligibility Gate runs before name discovery or broad web search.
- Fatal cases require another person's death within thirty days and selected-driver causation.
- A nonfatal exception requires both suspected serious injury or permanent impairment and objectively documented severe impairment.
- Measured BAC at or above 0.15 may satisfy the alcohol side of severe impairment; imputed BAC may not.
- Self-fatality-only, minor-injury, property-damage-only, and ordinary arrest cases are excluded.
- Automatic publication targets at least 99 percent precision.
- Restricted driver-register data and commercial people-search sources are excluded.
- Public-record requests require operator authorization.
- Imputed alcohol values are not treated as measured facts.

* Footnotes and Glossary

[fn:fars] Fatality Analysis Reporting System, or FARS: A national census of qualifying fatal motor-vehicle crashes maintained by the United States National Highway Traffic Safety Administration.

[fn:entity-resolution] Entity resolution: The process of deciding whether records from separate sources describe the same real-world person or event.

[fn:publication-gate] Publication gate: The only component authorized to convert a reviewed candidate into a canonical named identity link.

[fn:eligibility-gate] Eligibility gate: A deterministic decision step that blocks a record from downstream identity research until mandatory scope conditions are satisfied.
