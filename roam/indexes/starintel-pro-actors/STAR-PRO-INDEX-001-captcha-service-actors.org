* Current Transport Research Finding

Approved research favors:

- Sento mailboxes for tightly coupled same-process actors;
- RabbitMQ as the leading candidate for durable paid work crossing process/container/host boundaries;
- ZeroMQ deferred from the first paid-solve critical path until Common Lisp binding/runtime, persistence/replay, security, lifecycle, benchmark, and failure-injection evidence justify it.

This is an approved research conclusion, not an approved queue topology or transport architecture.

* Research-Derived Implementation Order Candidate

This ordering remains subject to design approval:

1. settle broker/orchestrator and transport-neutral protocol designs;
2. implement broker/fake provider locally with Sento;
3. if transport ADR is approved, add the durable RabbitMQ boundary and failure tests;
4. harden publication outcomes, confirm/return/reconnect handling, and durable attempt/outbox behavior;
5. implement exact provider adapters only from fixture-backed current capability contracts;
6. add provider health/circuit/cost/acceptance telemetry before automatic fallback;
7. reconsider ZeroMQ from measured evidence only.

* Non-Goals

- browser fingerprint fabrication as a generic subsystem feature;
- persistence of challenge tokens, provider credentials, proxy secrets, or authenticated session material in ordinary durable messages/documents;
- provider selection solely by lowest advertised price;
- treating research approval as design approval.
