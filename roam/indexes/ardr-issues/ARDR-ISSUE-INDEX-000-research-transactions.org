* Scope

Every GitHub issue that requests, contains, or directly governs an ARDR/ADARD research pass must have a durable tracked transaction under `roam/research/ardr-issues/`.

The transaction file does not replace a coherent canonical domain research node. It records issue/run scope, pass history, durable findings or decisions, and links to every canonical node refined or governed by the issue.

Pure design/implementation issues do not receive fake research transactions merely to satisfy bookkeeping; they link back to their source research transaction.

* Invariant

A research/research-control issue without its transaction file is incomplete. A transaction with no substantive pass content may seed work, but the worker may not claim a completed research pass until its findings/sources are persisted in tracked Org files.
