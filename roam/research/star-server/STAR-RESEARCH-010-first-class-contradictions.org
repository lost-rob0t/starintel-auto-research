* Research Question

Should StarIntel represent contradictions as first-class records rather than overwrite one claim with another?

* Finding

*KEEP.* Provenance systems already distinguish entities, activities, agents, derivation, attribution, and qualified relationships. RDF-star work also targets statements about statements. Together they support a model where two incompatible claims can coexist while each keeps its own source, time, confidence, extraction path, and support or contradiction relation.

For StarIntel, the research direction is to preserve both claims and model the conflict explicitly. Resolution can be a later inference result; it should not destroy the underlying evidence.

* Research Gaps

- Decide whether contradictions are relation documents, statement nodes, or assertion annotations.
- Define contradiction types: mutually exclusive, temporally inconsistent, source-disputed, and unresolved.
- Define when contradiction detection is deterministic versus inference-driven.

* Footnotes and Glossary

- *Contradiction*: Two assertions that cannot both hold under the same interpretation and validity conditions.
- *Qualified assertion*: A claim carrying provenance, time, confidence, or other contextual metadata.
