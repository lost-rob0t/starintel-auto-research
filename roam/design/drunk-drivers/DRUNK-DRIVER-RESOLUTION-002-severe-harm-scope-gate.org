* DONE Authority

This file is a normative amendment[fn:normative-amendment] to =DRUNK-DRIVER-RESOLUTION-001=. Where the earlier design permits a broader alcohol-relevance class to enter identity search, this approved scope gate takes precedence.

No implementation of the FARS-to-name pipeline is conformant unless it applies this gate before public web search, news search, archive lookup, court search, public-record planning, person extraction, or candidate identity creation.

* DONE Objective

Prevent the project from becoming a national list of ordinary impaired-driving arrests.

The named-case resolver is limited to:

1. impaired drivers whose conduct caused or materially contributed to another person's death within thirty days of the crash; or
2. a narrow nonfatal exception where the driver caused a suspected serious injury or permanent impairment and severe impairment is objectively documented.

* DONE Hard Eligibility Formula

#+begin_src text
eligible = fatal_causation
        OR (serious_injury_causation AND severe_impairment)
#+end_src

Each term is independently evidenced. Unknown is not true.

=fatal_causation= requires:

- at least one deceased person other than the selected driver;
- death within thirty days of the crash;
- evidence that the selected driver's conduct caused or materially contributed to the fatal crash;
- evidence that impairment is relevant to the selected driver and incident.

=serious_injury_causation= requires:

- at least one person other than the selected driver with =KABCO A=[fn:kabco], suspected serious injury, permanent impairment, life-threatening injury, or an equivalent supported classification;
- evidence that the selected driver's conduct caused or materially contributed to that injury.

=severe_impairment= requires at least one of:

- measured blood-alcohol concentration at or above 0.15 grams per deciliter;
- official toxicology plus an official finding of drug impairment;
- explicit police, prosecutor, court, or adjudicated evidence of severe or substantial impairment supported by testing or observed incapacity.

Imputed alcohol values, prior convictions, open-container records, test refusal alone, model inference, and arrest coding alone cannot satisfy =severe_impairment=.

* DONE Exclusions

The following are always outside the named identity-resolution pipeline unless later evidence changes the facts:

- property-damage-only impaired-driving incidents;
- minor or possible injury cases;
- ordinary driving-under-the-influence arrests without qualifying harm;
- checkpoint and traffic-stop cases without a qualifying crash outcome;
- license actions and prior-conviction datasets;
- crashes where only the selected driver died;
- crashes where another person's death or serious injury was caused by another driver or remains unsupported;
- cases supported only by imputed blood-alcohol concentration;
- allegations with no accessible incident facts;
- cases where the injury outcome is unknown and cannot be resolved within the eligibility budget.

Excluded records may remain in anonymous aggregate, enforcement, or public-health datasets. They do not receive named person records or =resolved_as= relations.

Add one actor to the earlier pipeline:

The =Severe Harm Eligibility Gate= is the twenty-seventh bounded actor in the domain design.

It runs before =Alcohol Relevance Classifier=. The classifier may refine eligible records but cannot admit a record rejected by this gate.

* DONE Actor Contract

** Severe Harm Eligibility Gate

Purpose: stop records that do not satisfy the fatal-causation or severe-nonfatal boundary before identity research begins.

Consumes:

- normalized crash, vehicle, person, injury, fatality, and alcohol records;
- official crash outcome fields;
- existing public legal or causation evidence when already available;
- versioned eligibility policy.

Produces:

- one terminal eligibility state;
- track: =fatal-causation=, =severe-nonfatal-exception=, or none;
- harm findings;
- causation findings;
- impairment findings;
- missing-proof requirements;
- exact exclusion reasons;
- policy version and input hash.

States:

- =eligible:fatal-causation=;
- =eligible:severe-nonfatal-exception=;
- =hold:causation-review=;
- =hold:outcome-unresolved=;
- =ineligible:self-fatality-only=;
- =ineligible:harm-threshold-not-met=;
- =ineligible:causation-not-supported=;
- =ineligible:impairment-threshold-not-met=;
- =ineligible:source-insufficient=.

Rules:

- unknown, missing, or contradictory evidence cannot be coerced to true;
- no downstream identity actor receives an =ineligible= branch;
- =hold= permits only bounded official outcome or causation retrieval;
- broad web and person-name search remain disabled during =hold=;
- the gate preserves allegation, charge, plea, verdict, conviction, dismissal, and correction status exactly;
- a later official correction may reopen the gate through a new versioned attempt;
- every decision is replayable from retained inputs and policy version.

* DONE Causation Evidence Policy

Strong causation evidence includes:

1. court judgment, plea, verdict, sentencing finding, or filed charging document;
2. prosecutor or district-attorney release;
3. police, sheriff, highway-patrol, or official crash report;
4. coroner, medical-examiner, or crash-reconstruction record;
5. two independent reputable reports directly attributing the causal claim to official records.

The system distinguishes:

- =caused=;
- =materially-contributed=;
- =alleged-caused=;
- =charged-caused=;
- =adjudicated-caused=;
- =causation-disputed=;
- =causation-unknown=.

Research eligibility may proceed on a strongly supported allegation, but public StarIntel output must preserve that procedural status and never rewrite an allegation as a conviction.

* DONE Search Restrictions

For =eligible= branches, the existing query portfolio and resolver may run normally.

For =hold= branches, permitted queries are limited to:

- official crash report or incident number;
- official fatality or injury outcome;
- prosecutor or court causation allegation;
- coroner or medical-examiner outcome;
- official toxicology needed to evaluate the exception.

For =ineligible= branches:

- zero person-name discovery queries;
- zero general news searches;
- zero archive searches;
- zero court-name searches;
- zero public-record request drafts;
- zero candidate person records;
- zero named public exports.

* DONE Output Contract

An excluded record produces a compact nonidentity decision:

#+begin_src json
{
  "protocol": "star.severe-harm-eligibility.v1",
  "case_id": "year:ST_CASE",
  "driver_id": "anonymous-driver-id",
  "state": "ineligible:harm-threshold-not-met",
  "track": null,
  "harm_to_other_person": false,
  "fatality_within_30_days": false,
  "serious_injury": false,
  "driver_causation": "unsupported",
  "severe_impairment": "not-evaluated",
  "search_permitted": false,
  "policy_version": 1,
  "evidence_ids": []
}
#+end_src

This document may be retained for replay and aggregate exclusion metrics. It is not a named accusation record.

An eligible branch carries the gate result into every downstream message. The Publication Gate must reject any identity packet whose eligibility proof is missing, invalid, superseded, or from another driver in the same crash.

* DONE Publication Gate Amendment

The existing Publication Gate receives three additional mandatory inputs:

- eligibility decision;
- harm and causation proof packet;
- eligibility policy version.

A =verified= identity requires:

1. eligible state;
2. same-driver continuity from eligibility through identity resolution;
3. no material contradiction in harm, causation, impairment, or role;
4. independent review of both identity and eligibility;
5. existing calibrated identity threshold and source-independence requirements.

Identity confidence cannot compensate for missing eligibility evidence.

* DONE Benchmark Amendments

Add mandatory fixtures:

1. Kearns fatal-causation positive;
2. impaired driver present in a fatal crash but not causally responsible;
3. selected driver self-fatality only;
4. fatality outside the thirty-day window;
5. severe injury plus measured BAC 0.15 or greater;
6. severe injury plus official drug impairment;
7. severe injury plus imputed-only BAC;
8. minor injury plus BAC 0.20;
9. property damage plus BAC 0.25;
10. serious injury caused by another driver;
11. unknown outcome that remains unresolved;
12. charge later dismissed or causation corrected.

Release gates:

- zero known ineligible cases reach identity search;
- zero self-fatality-only cases reach identity search;
- zero minor-injury or property-damage cases reach identity search;
- zero imputed-only cases pass the severe nonfatal exception;
- every eligible case has resolvable harm and causation evidence;
- exclusion decisions replay deterministically;
- search avoidance is measured and reported.

A single known gate-bypass publication blocks promotion.

* DONE Implementation Order

1. Define =star.severe-harm-eligibility.v1=.
2. Implement the deterministic gate before live search.
3. Add the twelve eligibility fixtures.
4. Add Kearns as the first fatal-causation positive.
5. Add explicit exclusion and hold outputs.
6. Carry the immutable gate result into every downstream message.
7. Amend the independent reviewer and publication gate.
8. Run gate-only tests before implementing Brave Search.
9. Include eligibility strata in the 100-case pilot.
10. Include gate bypass and false-exclusion analysis in the 200-case benchmark.

* DONE Acceptance Criteria

This amendment is implemented only when:

- the gate executes before all identity discovery;
- an omitted policy block fails closed;
- fatality to another person and selected-driver causation are independently checked;
- the severe nonfatal exception requires both serious injury causation and objectively documented severe impairment;
- imputed BAC cannot satisfy the exception;
- ineligible cases produce no named identity artifacts;
- hold cases cannot execute broad search;
- publication rejects a missing or mismatched eligibility packet;
- every required fixture passes;
- repository documentation and indexes identify this amendment as authoritative.
