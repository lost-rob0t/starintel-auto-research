* REVIEW Objective

Amend =STAR-RESEARCH-PIPELINE-003= so its ownership, messages, routing, scheduling, leases, replay, review, and completion semantics execute revisioned jurisdiction coverage without mutable logical-ID shortcuts, multi-writer state, ambiguous review heads, or time-insensitive completion.

This amendment preserves the parent supervision architecture. After approval it supersedes conflicting protocol-v1 fields and state rules.

* REVIEW Normative Protocol Identity

The envelope protocol is =star.government-data.v1.1=. Payload schemas are versioned separately, for example =ohio.coverage-run.v1.3= or =government-data.review-decision.v1=.

A field named =schema-version= inside a payload never changes the envelope protocol version. Commands and events must carry both:

- =protocol "star.government-data.v1.1"=
- =payload-schema-id= and =payload-schema-version=

* REVIEW Problems Corrected

The parent v1 design:

- carries logical source-profile IDs through commands, events, results, checkpoints, routing, and scheduling
- omits deployment, search-plan, subfamily-decision, gap, route-assessment, review-decision, and coverage-gate revisions
- has no serialized route-assessment or atomic completion owner
- has no independent immutable review record
- risks two authorities writing the same profile/assessment review state
- does not define a single linear current review head
- lacks deterministic gate hash, validity, and freshness semantics
- merges durable logical jobs with leases issued only after dispatch
- lacks per-attempt fencing tokens

* REVIEW Normative Status

This node defines protocol v1.1. Both =PIPELINE-003= and this amendment require approval before implementation. Protocol v1 remains readable for migration and historical replay only after cutover.

* REVIEW Authority Changes

Add pinned state owners:

- Source Deployment Authority
- Search Plan Authority
- Gap Authority
- Route Assessment Authority
- Coverage Gate Authority
- Independent Review Authority

Existing identity/profile authorities own subject revisions. Independent Review owns separate review-decision revisions. Coverage Gate owns aggregate gate revisions. Coverage Ledger stores observations/materialized views only.

Root readiness requires replay completion for identity, deployment, profile, search-plan, gap, route-assessment, coverage-gate, review, adapter registry, raw artifact, rate/lease, and coverage authorities.

* REVIEW Durable Document Families

Required families:

- government unit/component records
- deployment/profile/applicability/search-plan/gap/subfamily/route-assessment revisions
- independent review-decision revisions
- jurisdiction/wave/state coverage-gate revisions
- policy and manifest/runtime snapshots
- adapter-job revisions and attempt records
- retrieval events, artifacts, evidence, and checkpoints

Every subject transition is a new subject revision under one subject owner. Every human review transition is a separate review revision.

* REVIEW Independent Review Decision and Linear Head

#+begin_src lisp
(defstruct government-data-review-decision-revision
  review-id review-revision-id prior-review-revision-id
  supersedes-review-revision-ids
  subject-kind subject-revision-id decision
  reviewer-id reviewer-generation
  independence-policy-id conflict-check-result
  evidence-ids notes decided-at created-at)
#+end_src

Subject kinds include deployment, profile, applicability contract, policy snapshot, manifest set, runtime set, search plan, gap, subfamily decision, route assessment, and coverage gate.

Review rules:

- one stable =review-id= exists per subject revision
- its revisions form one linear compare-and-swap chain
- a commit supplies the expected current review head
- concurrent forks are rejected; they are never resolved by timestamp
- absence of a review head means unreviewed
- the unsuperseded head determines current review decision
- decisions are reviewed, changes-requested, rejected, or invalidated
- changes-requested/rejected normally cause a new subject revision; they do not rewrite the old subject
- review invalidation creates a new review revision and leaves subject history intact
- self-review and policy conflicts are rejected

No “latest applicable” timestamp query is allowed.

* REVIEW Atomic Coverage Gate and Canonical Basis

#+begin_src lisp
(defstruct government-data-coverage-gate-revision
  gate-id gate-revision-id prior-gate-revision-id
  supersedes-gate-revision-ids
  gate-kind scope-id run-id wave-id
  applicability-contract-id applicability-contract-version
  applicability-contract-review-revision-id
  policy-snapshot-id policy-review-revision-id
  adapter-manifest-set-hash manifest-set-review-revision-id
  runtime-environment-set-hash runtime-set-review-revision-id
  route-assessment-revision-ids route-assessment-review-revision-ids
  required-subject-review-revision-ids blocker-ids status
  basis-schema-version hash-algorithm basis-as-of basis-hash
  freshness-deadline valid-through
  decided-at decider-id created-at)
#+end_src

Gate kinds are jurisdiction completion, wave exit, and state completion. Only a reviewed passed jurisdiction gate may drive catalog =DONE=.

Canonical basis rules:

1. =basis-schema-version= identifies the exact canonical serialization.
2. Revision pairs are sorted by route order from the applicability contract, then subject kind, logical ID, and revision ID.
3. Strings are UTF-8 normalized, timestamps are UTC RFC 3339, absent fields are explicit nulls, and unordered maps are key-sorted.
4. The default algorithm is SHA-256 over the canonical byte sequence.
5. =basis-as-of= is the evaluation time used for freshness and validity.
6. =freshness-deadline= is the minimum current reassessment/expiry deadline among all bound subjects.
7. =valid-through= is the minimum of contract validity, policy validity, subject validity, and freshness deadline.

Current-completion materialization must reject a passed gate immediately when current time exceeds =valid-through= or when any bound review head becomes invalidated. An explicit expired gate revision is still emitted for history/notification, but current status does not wait for that write.

The Coverage Gate Authority validates the exact ordered sixteen-route basis and creates a new gate on evidence change, invalidation, expiry, or contract/policy migration. Independent Review separately reviews the gate.

* REVIEW Job and Attempt Separation

A durable job revision binds logical work, exact subject revisions, contract, policy, adapter/manifest/runtime versions, bounds, artifact policy, and idempotency key. It contains no future lease.

#+begin_src lisp
(defstruct government-data-adapter-attempt
  attempt-id attempt-number job-revision-id prior-attempt-id
  rate-lease-id rate-lease-generation rate-lease-fence
  rate-lease-key-hash worker-generation started-at deadline input-hash)
#+end_src

After job validation the dispatcher acquires a lease and creates an attempt. Retry creates a new attempt/fence. Logical scope change creates a new job revision.

* REVIEW Routing and Ownership

| Work | Serialization/read key |
|------|------------------------|
| subject writes | logical subject ID for one-writer serialization |
| subfamily decisions | unit + route + subfamily + contract version |
| route assessments | unit + route + contract version |
| coverage gates | gate kind + scope + contract/policy |
| review decisions | subject kind + subject revision ID |
| acquisition cursor | profile revision + partition |
| schedules | reviewed profile revision + policy snapshot |
| coverage materialization | scope + contract/policy + reviewed gate revision |

Subject authorities own subjects; Independent Review owns reviews; Coverage Gate owns gates; Coverage Ledger owns observations/derived views. No authority writes another authority’s canonical state.

* REVIEW Scheduler, Lease, Artifact, and Checkpoint Rules

Scheduled work pins reviewed profile/deployment revisions, policy, manifest/runtime hashes, checkpoint, due reason, and freshness basis. Changed review heads force cancellation/replan.

Rate/Lease issues ID, key hash, generation, fence, issue time, expiry, and close state. Every request carries the attempt fence.

Commit order is job validation, lease issue, attempt creation, request, artifact reserve/stream/hash, artifact/retrieval commit, evidence/proposals, checkpoint, lease close, terminal result.

Success is invalid with unexplained truncation, uncommitted artifacts, unreconciled counts, or expired fence.

* REVIEW Protocol Migration

- v1 logical-ID commands remain readable but cannot create current canonical decisions
- explicit migration selects exact revisions
- old versions become immutable revisions with aliases
- coverage observations do not become assessments/gates
- old gaps require first revisions and independent reviews
- old review fields do not become review decisions automatically
- no jurisdiction becomes complete without a new reviewed gate
- mixed v1/v1.1 is migration/read-only after cutover

* REVIEW Deterministic Fixtures

- envelope protocol versus payload schema mismatch rejection
- review compare-and-swap conflict rejection
- self-review rejection and invalidation preserving subject history
- canonical basis serialization/hash fixture
- equivalent unordered inputs producing identical hash
- different route order or null handling producing rejection
- gate current-status expiry without waiting for an expiry write
- route/review change after an old gate without old replay mutation
- coverage observations without reviewed gate cannot complete
- retry with same job/new attempt fence
- stale-fence and artifact/checkpoint ordering fixtures
- explicit v1 subject/review/gate migration

* REVIEW Acceptance Criteria

1. Envelope and payload versions are unambiguous.
2. Every canonical decision pins exact revisions.
3. Review streams are linear and compare-and-swap protected.
4. Subject, review, route-assessment, and gate owners are disjoint.
5. Gate basis hashing and validity are deterministic.
6. Current completion expires without waiting for a mutation.
7. Coverage Ledger cannot synthesize completion.
8. Jobs and attempts are separate/fenced.
9. Checkpoints cannot outrun artifacts/evidence.
10. v1 migration cannot create unreviewed completion.
