* Research Question

Should StarIntel relations carry temporal qualification so changing real-world relationships can be represented directly?

* Finding

*KEEP.* Temporal knowledge graphs exist because static triples lose information when facts change. Employment, ownership, membership, location, contracts, office-holding, and similar relationships often need a validity interval or event time separate from the time StarIntel collected the document.

A StarIntel relation therefore may need multiple clocks: when the relationship was valid, when the source asserted it, when StarIntel observed it, and when the record was processed. Conflating those timestamps would make historical reconstruction unreliable.

* Research Gaps

- Define valid-time versus observed-time semantics.
- Decide interval representation for unknown starts or ends.
- Define rules for overlapping or contradictory temporal assertions.

* Footnotes and Glossary

- *Valid time*: The period when a real-world assertion is understood to hold.
- *Observed time*: The time StarIntel acquired or recorded evidence for an assertion.
