* Research Question

Should StarIntel store unanswered questions as first-class records that can drive future research actors?

* Finding

*EXPERIMENT.* Open-world knowledge-graph work distinguishes unknown facts from known false facts, and automated hypothesis-generation work uses graph structure to generate candidate questions or hypotheses. A durable question document is not a standard primitive, but it is compatible with that research direction.

A useful StarIntel experiment would store the target claim, known evidence, missing evidence, status, priority, provenance, and actor-generated follow-up tasks. The main risk is backlog noise: question records are useful only if they close, merge, expire, or produce measurable research progress.

* Research Gaps

- Define question lifecycle: OPEN, BLOCKED, ANSWERED, DISPROVEN, EXPIRED, MERGED.
- Measure whether question persistence improves completion rate.
- Prevent trivial or duplicate questions from flooding the graph.
