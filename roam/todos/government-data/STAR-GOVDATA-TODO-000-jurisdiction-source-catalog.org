* Completion Contract

A jurisdiction TODO reaches =DONE= only after a reviewed source profile records all discovered routes, negative findings, and unresolved gaps. Search results are candidates until official ownership or delegation is verified.

** Required source routes

1. Official identity, home page, canonical domain, aliases, and delegated vendor domains.
2. Open-data catalogs, data.json, DCAT, CKAN, Socrata, ArcGIS Hub, Huwise/OpenDataSoft, and downloadable bulk files.
3. GIS, parcel maps, ArcGIS services, OGC services, geospatial downloads, and map viewers.
4. Legislation, charter, code, ordinances, resolutions, and legal publishing systems.
5. Meetings, calendars, agendas, packets, minutes, votes, transcripts, recordings, and video archives.
6. Budgets, annual financial reports, audits, checkbooks, payroll, debt, grants, and revenue records.
7. Procurement, bids, solicitations, awards, contracts, purchase orders, and vendor-payment systems.
8. Property, assessment, tax, recorder, deed, parcel, and land-record systems where publicly available.
9. Courts, dockets, case indexes, and clerk systems where public access is authorized.
10. Elections, candidates, campaign finance, precincts, results, and voter-information systems.
11. Public safety, incident, dispatch, jail, inspection, and enforcement datasets where lawfully public.
12. Permits, licenses, planning, zoning, development, environmental, and code-enforcement systems.
13. Public-records or FOIA portal, request policy, retention schedules, contacts, and fee rules.
14. RSS/Atom feeds, alerts, newsletters, sitemaps, robots directives, APIs, exports, and change feeds.
15. Web archives, retired domains, historical portals, document repositories, and source migrations.
16. For every route, record platform family, vendor, endpoints, authentication, rate limits, pagination, refresh behavior, coverage dates, raw-artifact policy, provenance locators, and known gaps.

** Required output fields

- official status and verification evidence
- source-profile identifier
- data family and jurisdiction component
- platform and vendor fingerprints
- acquisition method and preferred adapter
- stable locator and discovery path
- access, credential, rate, and legal constraints
- earliest and latest observed coverage
- raw capture and content hash policy
- incremental cursor or reconciliation method
- unsupported, missing, inaccessible, or records-request-required state
- reviewer and review timestamp
