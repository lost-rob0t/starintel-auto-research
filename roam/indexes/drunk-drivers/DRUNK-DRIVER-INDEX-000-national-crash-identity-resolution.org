* Purpose

This index tracks the StarIntel national impaired-driving crash identity-resolution project.

The project keeps official anonymous crash data separate from named public-case records, then links them only through evidence-backed, independently reviewed resolution claims.

* Current Decisions

- FARS is the national fatal-crash backbone.
- Anonymous FARS people are never renamed in place.
- Public-source identity resolution is probabilistic and may abstain.
- Event, driver role, and person identity are separate proofs.
- Automatic publication targets at least 99 percent precision.
- Restricted driver-register data and commercial people-search sources are excluded.
- Public-record requests require operator authorization.
- Imputed alcohol values are not treated as measured facts.

* Footnotes and Glossary

[fn:fars] Fatality Analysis Reporting System, or FARS: A national census of qualifying fatal motor-vehicle crashes maintained by the United States National Highway Traffic Safety Administration.

[fn:entity-resolution] Entity resolution: The process of deciding whether records from separate sources describe the same real-world person or event.

[fn:publication-gate] Publication gate: The only component authorized to convert a reviewed candidate into a canonical named identity link.
