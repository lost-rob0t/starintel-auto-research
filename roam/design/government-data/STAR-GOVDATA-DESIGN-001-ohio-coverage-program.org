* REVIEW Objective

Make Ohio the first state executed from the generated jurisdiction catalog.

The program produces immutable subject revisions, independent review-decision revisions, and atomic coverage-gate revisions for the State of Ohio and all 88 counties. It records authority, source identity, methods, temporal/jurisdictional scope, refresh, provenance, applicability, gaps, and historical source relationships.

This is a bounded design. It does not claim Ohio coverage is complete and does not implement production runtime.

* REVIEW Architecture Decision

Ohio is one state-scoped run over reusable actors and adapters. Government units/components are durable records and bounded job keys, not permanent actors.

Required durable authorities before live work:

- Government Identity Authority
- Source Deployment and Source Profile authorities
- Search Plan, Gap, Route Assessment, and Coverage Gate authorities
- Independent Review Authority
- Raw Artifact Authority and retrieval-event ledger
- Provenance Validator and Coverage Ledger
- Official Domain Resolver and Platform Fingerprint pools
- Adapter Registry and bounded workers
- Rate and Lease Authority with attempt fencing

Subject authorities never write human review decisions. The review authority never mutates subjects. The Coverage Gate Authority creates aggregate gate subjects; the Independent Review Authority separately reviews them.

A live wave cannot begin until exact contract/policy/manifest/runtime snapshots, durable authorities, and deterministic fixtures are ready.

* REVIEW Parent Protocol Requirement

=STAR-RESEARCH-PIPELINE-004= formalizes the revision-aware amendment required by this program. It must be approved and reconciled with =STAR-RESEARCH-PIPELINE-003= before implementation.

* REVIEW Scope

Included:

- State of Ohio and all 88 county GEOIDs
- official/delegated state, county, component, shared, and regional systems
- the sixteen-route contract in =STAR-GOVDATA-DESIGN-002=
- Franklin County as first complete pilot
- fingerprint-driven diverse-county validation
- visible predecessor, successor, migration, archive, and temporal relationships in every wave
- explicit positive, partial, negative, restricted, retired, and not-applicable decisions
- immutable independent reviews and atomic jurisdiction/wave gates

Deferred:

- exhaustive municipality, township, school, and special-district coverage
- production crawling/normalization
- automatic records-request sending
- credential creation, terms acceptance, payments, or access-control bypass
- unauthorized protected-data acquisition
- exhaustive historical crawling beyond bounded policy
- continuous scheduling before pilot acceptance

* REVIEW Ohio Identity and Source Seeds

The identity set contains the State of Ohio, 88 counties, and discovered components including auditors, recorders, clerks, election boards, prosecutors, sheriffs, engineers, treasurers, courts, commissions, health districts, and planning bodies.

Each component records parent, function, authority, service geography, evidence, confidence, and county-owned/elected/shared/regional/municipal/state/delegated applicability.

| Source family | Candidate authority | Required assessment |
|---------------+---------------------+---------------------|
| Ohio portal | =https://ohio.gov/= | agency/domain/delegation relationships |
| Ohio Laws | =https://codes.ohio.gov/= | current/prior/future-effective authenticated versions |
| Legislature | =https://www.legislature.ohio.gov/= | bills, sponsors, committees, votes, calendars, journals, documents |
| OhioBuys | state procurement surfaces | public solicitations, awards, contracts, attachments, capability boundaries |
| Ohio Checkbook | =https://checkbook.ohio.gov/= | participation scope, periods, exports, reconciliation gaps |
| OGRIP/OSIP/LBRS | official GIS surfaces | imagery, elevation, county files, services, archives |
| Secretary of State | =https://www.ohiosos.gov/= | elections, campaign finance, boards, result states |
| Ohio courts | Supreme Court surfaces | state systems, statistics, local-court directory |
| Auditor of State | official Auditor surfaces | audits, reports, findings, filing periods |
| Archives/retention | official state surfaces | schedules, transferred records, indexes |
| Public-records/open-meetings law | Ohio Laws | effective versions, exemptions, covered bodies |

Every promoted source requires authority evidence. Ohio Checkbook participation is discovery/reconciliation evidence only.

* REVIEW Route and History Contracts

All route/subfamily semantics come from one pinned, independently reviewed applicability-contract revision. State substitution is allowed only when the rule permits it and scope proves the county/component/period.

Every wave records first/last observations, predecessor/successor locators, redirects, migrations, retired-domain evidence, archive candidates, visible date bounds, and current/prior/future-effective distinctions.

* REVIEW Execution Waves

** Wave 0: Identity, legal baseline, and fingerprint census

1. Load Ohio and county GEOIDs.
2. Verify state identity/domains.
3. Capture records, meetings, retention, and archival authorities.
4. Create unresolved county/component candidates without promotion.
5. Collect bounded platform, vendor, delegation, service, court, and health fingerprints.
6. Record minimal historical identity.

A reviewed =:passed= Wave 0 gate binds the exact identity/legal/fingerprint subject and review revisions. No county is complete.

** Wave 1: State source systems

Profile Ohio Laws, Legislature, OhioBuys, Ohio Checkbook, OGRIP/OSIP/LBRS, Secretary of State, Supreme Court, Auditor of State, and archival/retention systems.

A reviewed =:passed= Wave 1 gate requires:

- reviewed profile/deployment revisions or reviewed blocked/unsupported decisions
- ranked methods bound to adapter manifest/runtime revisions
- rate, pagination, file, browser, auth, scope, and artifact policy
- raw evidence for promoted revisions
- county-indexed crosswalks where exposed
- Checkbook not used as finance completion
- minimal historical identity

** Wave 2: Franklin County pilot

Franklin County GEOID =39049= is the first complete end-to-end county pilot.

The Coverage Gate Authority creates one Franklin jurisdiction-completion gate revision binding:

- all sixteen exact route-assessment revisions
- the independent review revision for every route assessment and required subject
- independently reviewed applicability contract
- one policy snapshot and adapter manifest/runtime set
- exact profile, deployment, search-plan, subfamily-decision, gap, evidence, and artifact revisions
- negative-finding freshness at gate decision time
- basis hash over the ordered bound revision set

The gate may be =:passed= only when:

- every required/triggered subfamily has a reviewed decision
- no nonterminal state remains
- not-applicable has positive authority/service evidence
- negative states have completed reviewed search plans and current reviewed gaps
- every adapter result pins job revision, attempt, version, manifest/runtime hash, and lease fence
- deterministic replay reproduces the basis hash
- unexplained truncation is zero
- unresolved required manual review is zero

An independent reviewer then reviews the gate revision. Only a reviewed passed gate counts as Franklin completion.

** Wave 3: Fingerprint-driven diverse-county pilot

Initial candidates: Cuyahoga =39035=, Hamilton =39061=, Lucas =39095=, Delaware =39041=, Athens =39009=, Vinton =39163=, and Hancock =39063=.

Final selection maximizes platform/vendor, hosting authority, publication maturity, office fragmentation, shared services, court/health topology, geography/size, and archive/migration variation. A mid-sized non-large-metro or western/northwestern agricultural case remains required unless evidence proves equivalent coverage.

Each county requires its own reviewed passed jurisdiction gate. A separate reviewed Wave 3 gate binds those county gate revisions plus selection evidence and metrics.

Wave 3 passes only when:

- selected county gates pass Franklin rules
- no county schema fork is needed
- variation choices are evidence-backed
- reusable platform families cover at least 70% of supported successful endpoints
- one-off adapters have reusable semantics/fixtures
- replay succeeds for every subject, review, and gate revision
- unexplained truncation/stale-lease acceptance is zero
- duplicate deployments are reconciled
- manual-review and unresolved-route metrics satisfy a predeclared policy ceiling
- negative results are current

** Wave 4: All 88 counties

Use at most eight active county runs. Batch order is a versioned policy respecting host/vendor/deployment/tenant/key/credential/browser leases.

Every county requires a reviewed passed jurisdiction gate. State completion requires a reviewed state-completion gate binding all 88 current county gates under one contract/policy snapshot.

** Wave 5: Historical acquisition and reconciliation

Expand archives, reconcile predecessors/successors, establish refresh/retirement schedules, define tombstones/replacements, reconcile prior/current captures, and acquire bounded historical content.

Historical changes create new subject/review/gate revisions; old gates remain replayable.

* REVIEW Court and Negative Scope

County court coverage binds county courts, clerks, and authoritative linked local-court assessment revisions. No unified API does not mean not-applicable.

| Freshness class | Maximum default age | Typical routes |
|-----------------+---------------------+----------------|
| high | 30 days | feeds, meetings, procurement, permits, operational metadata |
| medium | 90 days | open data, finance, elections, public safety, public records |
| low | 180 days | identity, GIS, property, courts, archives |
| legal-versioned | next effective date or 180 days | law/legal policy |

Stale evidence expires the current gate basis and requires new search, gap, decision, assessment, review, and gate revisions.

* REVIEW Acceptance Criteria

1. All 88 counties are in scope.
2. All sixteen routes use one reviewed versioned applicability contract.
3. Subject owners and review authority are disjoint.
4. Route decisions pin immutable subject revisions and independent reviews.
5. Franklin/county/wave/state results use atomic reviewed gates with basis hashes.
6. Historical identity is captured in every wave.
7. Checkbook does not imply finance completeness.
8. Court results bind linked assessments.
9. Negative findings expire.
10. Adapter success cannot hide truncation, stale leases, or missing artifacts.
11. No permanent county actor exists.
12. =PIPELINE-004= is approved before implementation.

* REVIEW Deterministic Fixtures

Required fixtures include all route definitions, shared providers, legitimate/rejected not-applicable, stale negative evidence, oversized files, ArcGIS viewer/REST preference, effective-dated law, public-read/login-write procurement, linked courts, migrations, partial periods, browser/OCR boundaries, stale lease fences, artifact-before-checkpoint recovery, independent review conflicts, full Franklin basis hash, route change after old gate, gate expiry, and gate supersession.
