* Completion Contract

A jurisdiction reaches =DONE= only when one reviewed completion decision binds:

- one approved applicability-contract ID and version
- one policy snapshot
- reviewed immutable route-assessment revisions for all sixteen routes
- reviewed subfamily-decision revisions for every required or triggered conditional subfamily
- exact source-profile and deployment revisions
- completed search-plan revisions for negative results
- reviewed gap revisions with current reassessment deadlines
- immutable evidence and raw-artifact references
- reviewer identity and review timestamp

=:candidate-found=, =:ambiguous=, =:unassessed=, stale negative findings, mutable logical IDs, unresolved required manual review, or missing applicability evidence block =DONE=.

A route may end in a contract-permitted reviewed positive, partial, negative, access-restricted, retired, or not-applicable state. =:not-applicable= requires positive authority or service-topology evidence. Search failure alone never makes a route not applicable.

The operational-metadata route is cross-cutting. It is complete only when every bound source, deployment, search plan, decision, artifact policy, and gap contains the required platform, access, rate, pagination, cursor, refresh, scope, provenance, truncation, and reassessment metadata.

* Required Source Routes

1. Official identity, canonical domains, aliases, components, delegated hosting, and service-provider topology.
2. Open-data catalogs, data.json, DCAT, CKAN, Socrata, ArcGIS Hub, Huwise/OpenDataSoft, exports, and reviewed absence.
3. GIS, parcel maps, ArcGIS/OGC services, downloads, viewers, and responsible-provider mapping.
4. Charter, code, ordinances, resolutions, laws, policies, and authoritative version relationships.
5. Meetings, calendars, agendas, packets, minutes, votes, transcripts, recordings, and video archives.
6. Budgets, financial reports, audits, checkbooks, payroll, debt, grants, revenue, and scope gaps.
7. Procurement, bids, solicitations, awards, contracts, purchase orders, attachments, and vendor payments.
8. Property, assessment, tax, recorder, deed, parcel, and land-record systems or resolved providers.
9. Court and clerk topology, dockets, case indexes, documents, access restrictions, and linked local-court assessments.
10. Elections, candidates, campaign finance, precincts, results states, and voter-information products.
11. Sheriff, jail, incident, dispatch, inspection, enforcement, and public-safety systems where lawfully public.
12. Building, zoning, planning, development, health, environmental, licensing, and code-enforcement providers and systems.
13. Public-records portal or method, policy, retention schedules, contacts, fees, and official escalation instructions.
14. Sitemaps, RSS/Atom/iCalendar, alerts, newsletters, APIs, exports, and change feeds or reviewed absence.
15. Archives, retired domains, historical portals, repositories, migrations, predecessor/successor links, and visible temporal bounds.
16. Cross-cutting operational metadata for every source, decision, gap, artifact, and acquisition path.

* Required Output Bindings

- applicability-contract and policy-snapshot identifiers
- assessment, subfamily-decision, source-profile, deployment, search-plan, and gap revision IDs
- official status and authority evidence
- data family, component, and service geography
- platform, vendor, tenant, adapter version, and manifest hash
- locator, discovery path, predecessor/successor, and archive evidence
- access, credential, rate-lease, legal, privacy, and browser constraints
- earliest/latest observed coverage and completeness scope
- raw-artifact, retrieval-event, and content-hash references
- cursor/checkpoint and reconciliation method
- unsupported, missing, inaccessible, partial, retired, records-request, OCR, or manual-review state
- freshness class and reassessment deadline
- independent reviewer and review timestamp
