* Research Seed

Verify and rank current public threat-intelligence sources, including CISA KEV, FIRST EPSS, NVD CVE API 2.0, MITRE ATT&CK TAXII/STIX, ThreatFox, URLhaus, MalwareBazaar metadata, Feodo Tracker, and the MISP public/default feed registry. Record endpoints, formats, authentication, rate limits, cadence, incremental cursor/checkpoint strategy, stable IDs, expiry/revocation, licensing, expected volume, trust/signal, overlap, provenance, and implementation wave.

The production direction is Star-Lang actor-native. Upstream Python/JS/shell tooling may inform protocol research but is not the implementation architecture. Malware binaries are out of scope; MalwareBazaar starts metadata/hash/signature-only.
